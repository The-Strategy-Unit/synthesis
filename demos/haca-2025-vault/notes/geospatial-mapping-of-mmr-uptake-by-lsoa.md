# Geospatial mapping of MMR uptake by LSOA

Geospatial mapping allows for the visualisation of MMR uptake at a granular level, such as Lower Layer Super Output Area (LSOA). Using data from the Federated Data Platform (FDP), analysts can map the number of children aged 4–17 who received one dose of MMR across different LSOAs. This approach helps identify geographic clusters of low uptake, enabling targeted public health interventions in specific neighbourhoods rather than broad areas.
