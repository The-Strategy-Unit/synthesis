# Community Pharmacy Workforce Planning Model

The Community Pharmacy Workforce Planning Model is a system developed at NHS England to inform planning for services delivered in high street pharmacy premises, such as Boots or Tesco. The model specifically focuses on two key professions regulated by the General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC): pharmacists and pharmacy technicians. Its scope is limited to community pharmacies, excluding hospital, secondary, or general practitioner settings, distinguishing it from the broader NHS long-term workforce planning.

The model utilises full-time equivalents (FTE) as its primary unit of currency. It incorporates data engineering steps, production projections over configured periods, and automated plotting for scenario comparison. Designed for flexibility, the model allows for updates as new data layers are integrated, moving away from legacy Excel-based workflows toward more scalable Python pipelines.
