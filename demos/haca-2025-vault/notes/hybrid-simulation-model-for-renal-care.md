# Hybrid simulation model for renal care

A hybrid modeling approach combining system dynamics and discrete event simulation to represent the full pathway of renal care, from chronic kidney disease (CKD) progression to kidney replacement therapy (KRT). Developed by the Strategy Unit, led by Lucy Morgan and Sally Thompson, the model addresses concerns from the Midlands Kidney Operational Delivery Network regarding future in-centre dialysis capacity. The system dynamics component models population-level flows and disease progression rates, while the discrete event simulation component tracks individual patient movements through KRT pathways, including in-centre dialysis, home dialysis, transplantation, and conservative management. This sequential hybrid structure allows for the testing of upstream interventions, such as slowing CKD progression, and downstream interventions, such as changes in KRT service delivery, in a risk-free environment to generate evidence-led business cases for system changes.
