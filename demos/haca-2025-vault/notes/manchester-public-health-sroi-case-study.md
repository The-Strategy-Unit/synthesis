# Manchester Public Health SROI Case Study

Manchester City Council applied the SROI methodology to evaluate public health projects, specifically focusing on a commissioned organization working in mental health and youth work. The project involved a limited number of stakeholders, which simplified engagement, and the organization provided detailed monthly quantitative data, making inputs and outputs easy to define. However, outcome analysis was challenging due to the lack of typical service users and the variability of factors like duration of change, dead weight, attribution, and drop-off. Central tendencies like mean, median, and mode are rarely quantified in these fields.

Despite these challenges, stakeholders worked creatively with their data and expertise to develop credible 'best guess' measures. The evaluation of the first year proved valuable for developing a forecast model to strengthen the business case for years two to four, leading to further investment decisions, stopping some actions, and increasing successful interventions.
