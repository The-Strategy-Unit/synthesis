# Right Care Right Person initiative in Suffolk

Right Care Right Person is an initiative implemented across police forces in England and Wales designed to redirect calls for service that are not best handled by the police, particularly those relating to mental health issues rather than crime. In Suffolk, the initiative was phased in starting in October 2023. Initially, it focused on 'concern for welfare' calls. Subsequent phases included calls regarding walkouts from healthcare facilities and the transportation of patients under Section 136 of the Mental Health Act, aiming to refer these to more appropriate agencies instead of using police vehicles or resources.

The initiative was evaluated by the Suffolk Office of Data and Analytics (SODA), a function of local government funded equally by local authorities, Suffolk Police, and the Integrated Care System (ICS) in Suffolk. SODA was commissioned jointly by Suffolk Constabulary and the ICS to assess the impact of the initiative on partner agencies, specifically addressing concerns that non-police services might be inundated by redirected demand.
