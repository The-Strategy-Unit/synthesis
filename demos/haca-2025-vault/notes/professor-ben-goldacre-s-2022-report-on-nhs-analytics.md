# Professor Ben Goldacre's 2022 Report on NHS Analytics

Professor Ben Goldacre’s 2022 report, titled 'Better, Broader, Safer', served as a review for the Secretary of State concerning NHS data infrastructure and usage. It is cited as a foundational document for HACA. The report contained numerous recommendations aimed at improving analytical practices within the NHS, including the critical need to invest in the development of analysts, the adoption of reproducible analytical pipelines (RAPs) as a guiding principle, and the open sharing of analysis across the health service. It specifically recommended holding a conference to facilitate these goals and foster a collaborative analytical community.

The review advocated for consolidating data dissemination around trusted research environments rather than distributing data to multiple locations. This recommendation was adopted into policy, although its delivery has encountered challenges. The review also promoted open working practices, which have been partially adopted and are increasingly becoming the norm within the sector.

A significant portion of the review focused on building capacity within the analytic community. Goldacre argued that while individual analytical work is valuable, it is often lost without supporting structures such as training programmes, textbooks, and conferences. The review highlighted the need for a more mature professional ecosystem to retain and scale expertise. However, the government did not adopt the recommendations regarding the structuring of the analytic community with the same energy as the infrastructure recommendations.
