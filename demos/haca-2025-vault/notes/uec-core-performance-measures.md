# UEC Core Performance Measures

The four primary UEC performance measures monitored in the Shropshire dashboard are: 1) 4-hour performance (time spent in emergency departments), 2) 12 hours in department, 3) ambulance handover times, and 4) Category 2 response mean time. These metrics provide operational staff with rapid visibility into emergency performance pressures. The dashboard allows monitoring of these measures against targets, plans, and previous periods (weekend, month, quarter, previous year). Additional metrics include length of stay and discharge length of stay, monitored on weekly and monthly bases to provide broader insights into system flow and efficiency.
