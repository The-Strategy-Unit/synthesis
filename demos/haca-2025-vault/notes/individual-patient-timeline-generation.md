# Individual patient timeline generation

This technique automates the creation of individual-level patient timelines to visualise interactions with the health system in the year preceding death. Unlike broad aggregate graphs, these timelines provide a detailed view of a single patient's journey, including physical and mental health prescriptions, scheduled care contacts, and missed appointments. The timelines are interactive, allowing users to hover over specific events—such as ambulance calls or emergency department attendances—to view detailed information like the subject of the call or reason for attendance.

Applied to all deaths in the dataset using NHS numbers, this method offers a more granular perspective on potential prevention opportunities and care pathways. There is particular interest in whether missing mental health appointments serves as a risk factor for suicide, especially in the context of policy discussions regarding charges for missed appointments. While previous research from Scotland suggested a link between missed appointments and suicide risk, definitive conclusions from this specific dataset were not drawn due to various data caveats.
