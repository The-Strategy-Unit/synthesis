# Patient advisory group feedback on waiting times

Engagement with a patient advisory group provided valuable context for interpreting quantitative results. Members expressed surprise at the lack of significant demographic disparities in waiting times, as their lived experiences suggested larger gaps, particularly for older adults and lower socioeconomic groups. They highlighted the negative impacts of waiting on physical, mental, and economic well-being, noting that many patients turned to private care. The group also identified critical gaps in support and communication, emphasising the need for regular updates to ensure patients do not feel forgotten and to promote 'waiting well' strategies.

This feedback underscores the importance of incorporating patient perspectives into research and policy development. While quantitative data may not always reveal significant disparities, the qualitative experiences of patients highlight the real-world consequences of waiting and the need for improved support systems. Integrating these insights can help create more patient-centred approaches to managing waiting lists and improving overall care experiences.
