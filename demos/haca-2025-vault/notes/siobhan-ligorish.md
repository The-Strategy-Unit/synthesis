# Siobhan Ligorish

Siobhan Ligorish is the Head of Data Visualisation at Kent and Medway Integrated Care Board (ICB). She played a key role in implementing the Woodyard Model within the organisation, aiming to reduce waste and improve flow within the business intelligence function. Ligorish presented the implementation and its outcomes at HACA 2025, detailing how the model helped transition the team from isolated efforts to a shared pathway for data processing and visualisation. Her work highlights the importance of cross-functional collaboration and explicit role definition in achieving efficient data operations.

In a separate HACA session, Ligorish described moving to the UK from Grenada in 2017 with experience in graphic design, government, and monitoring and evaluation. She said that barriers to recognition of her international qualifications led her to enter the NHS at Band 4. She subsequently progressed to a Band 7 role at Northumbria Healthcare before moving into data architecture and visualisation. She advocates a proactive approach to career advancement, encouraging analysts to apply for roles they feel confident in even when that means skipping intermediate bands.
