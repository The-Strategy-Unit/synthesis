# NHS Net Zero Commitment and Strategic Alignment

The NHS is legally committed to reaching net zero under the Health and Care Act 2022. Net zero involves reducing emissions as much as possible, with offsetting reserved for emissions that are impossible to remove. The trust views a greener NHS as a better healthcare system, linking sustainability with efficient resource use, care delivery close to home, and prevention of hospitalization. These goals align with the NHS's 10-year plan and the broader theme of future health and care systems. The trust encourages experts to integrate environmental measures into their analysis, recognizing that all healthcare activity has an associated carbon cost that must be spent wisely.
