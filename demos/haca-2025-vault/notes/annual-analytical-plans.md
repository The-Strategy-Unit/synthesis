# Annual Analytical Plans

Structured plans outlining the analysis to be conducted over a year, used to protect dedicated time for learning and development. Even in operational teams working at the frontline, creating these plans helps balance tight deadlines with the need for upskilling. The plans are supported by leadership development, which enables better stakeholder management and team engagement.

An annual program of work aligns analytical efforts with organizational priorities, such as patient outcomes, waiting lists, and primary care access, rather than focusing on individual dashboard requests. This plan defines intelligence questions, measurement criteria, and impact assessment for supported programs. It includes quarterly reviews with stakeholders to confirm progress, adjust timelines, and manage expectations. The operating model typically allocates 60-80% of time to routine and strategic questions, while 20-30% is reserved for urgent responses, ideally supported by self-service tools.
