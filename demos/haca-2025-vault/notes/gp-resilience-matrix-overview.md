# GP Resilience Matrix Overview

The GP Resilience Matrix is a strategic tool developed by NHS Kenton Medway Integrated Care Board (ICB) to provide targeted primary care support. It was created to address increasing variation in practice resilience, specifically regarding staffing gaps, demand pressures, operational strain, and general health outcomes. The tool aims to identify practices nearing risk or a 'tipping point' before they fail, allowing for early intervention and bespoke care packages. It is explicitly defined as neither a benchmarking tool nor a performance management tool, but rather a mechanism to improve flow visibility and decision-making through simple analytics. The tool evolved from an Excel-based proof of concept to a PowerBI dashboard to enhance user experience, flexibility, and visualisation capabilities.
