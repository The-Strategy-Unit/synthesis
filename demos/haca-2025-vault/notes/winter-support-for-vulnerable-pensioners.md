# Winter Support for Vulnerable Pensioners

A 15-month project delivered by DICE focused on targeted winter support for financially vulnerable pensioners in Dorset. The initiative aimed to identify residents at greater risk during winter months due to health, financial, or social vulnerabilities, prompted by national changes that significantly reduced pension credit eligibility. Analysis identified that one in five pensioners, approximately 44,000 residents, were exposed to financial vulnerability during the winter of 2023–2024. This figure was derived by comparing the 200,000 residents eligible for winter fuel payments in 2023 against the expected 20,000 eligible for pension credit in 2024, highlighting a significant population not accessing existing support mechanisms.

The project sought to proactively pinpoint individuals needing support to prevent crises, recognizing that cold weather exacerbates health conditions and increases demand on care services. Strategies included sending household support fund letters to high-risk cohorts, prioritizing patients on waiting lists, and increasing awareness of community spaces such as libraries. The goal was to shift outcomes and reduce pressure on the overstretched system through focused, data-driven targeting rather than spreading support thinly.
