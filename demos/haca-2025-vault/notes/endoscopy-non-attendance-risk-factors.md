# Endoscopy non-attendance risk factors

Analysis of endoscopy appointment data from 2022 to 2024 across five sites in Northumbria Healthcare Foundation Trust revealed an overall non-attendance rate of 3.9%, excluding cancellations. Several key risk factors were identified through this analysis.

Previous non-attendance is a strong predictor, showing an almost linear correlation where each additional missed hospital appointment increases the probability of future non-attendance. Deprivation, measured using the Index of Multiple Deprivation (IMD) score, also demonstrates a strong linear correlation; higher levels of deprivation in a patient's postcode correspond to higher non-attendance rates. Demographic variations are significant, with male patients in their 30s exhibiting roughly seven times the non-attendance rate of female patients in their 60s. High-risk patients tended to be younger and from lower IMD quintiles (1 or 2).
