# British Orthopaedic Association Standards for Trauma Compliance

The British Orthopaedic Association (BOA) guidelines set standards of care for patients following acute soft tissue or bone injuries. Key standards audited in this context include seeing patients within 72 hours of presentation, ensuring clinics are consultant-led, and providing patient leaflets. Compliance with these standards is used to evaluate the quality of outpatient fracture clinics. The audit at North Middlesex Hospital highlighted difficulties in meeting these guidelines prior to the virtual fracture clinic implementation, particularly regarding the 72-hour target and the provision of accessible patient information.
