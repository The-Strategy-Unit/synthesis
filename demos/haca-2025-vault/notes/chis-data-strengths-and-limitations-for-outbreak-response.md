# CHIS Data Strengths and Limitations for Outbreak Response

The Child Health Information System (CHIS) provides data on the number of children who received zero doses of vaccination by LSOA. Unlike the FTP dashboard, CHIS does not provide a denominator (total eligible children) by LSOA. However, in outbreak scenarios where the goal is to identify areas with the greatest need, having the absolute number of unvaccinated children is often more useful than a percentage. Access to multiple data sources allows analysts to select the most appropriate metric for their specific question, compensating for the limitations of individual systems.
