# Limitations of short-term endoscopy demand models

The short-term endoscopy demand and capacity model has several identified limitations that affect its generalizability. The model was tuned based on data from a single local trust, Torbay and South Devon Foundation Trust (referred to as 'Torby and SEN trust' in the transcript), which limits its applicability to other settings without substantial retuning. Simplifications in the model include the exclusion of ERCP and sigmoidoscopy procedures, as well as factors such as rebooking and patient non-attendance. Parameter sensitivity is noted as a key constraint, requiring careful calibration for different operational contexts to ensure accurate predictions.
