# Tripartite Leadership Team for Model Rollout

A tripartite leadership structure was adopted to manage the delivery of the demand capacity model, recognizing that no single individual possessed all necessary skills for decision-making. The team comprised three distinct roles: Karen Bradley, providing operational delivery and hospital workflow expertise; Chris Beeley, contributing data science expertise; and Jake Parsons, handling analytics, specifically regarding manual demand and capacity work.
