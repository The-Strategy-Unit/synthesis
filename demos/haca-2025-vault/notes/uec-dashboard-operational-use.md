# UEC Dashboard Operational Use

The dashboard was originally established to support Tier 1 escalation and Urgent and Emergency Care (UEC) reporting, providing near-live operational data for decision-making. It is utilized daily by UEC employees in executive or operational roles across the system, with access managed via a role-based platform in Power BI. Access has also been extended to NHS England colleagues, including regional UEC teams. The dashboard facilitates quick insights into performance and statistical variation for four main UEC core measures. It supports the creation of bespoke SPC (Statistical Process Control) comparison pages and includes a dedicated quality page. The tool has been recognized by national and regional teams and played a pivotal role in supporting the Integrated Care Board's (ICB) exit from national oversight.
