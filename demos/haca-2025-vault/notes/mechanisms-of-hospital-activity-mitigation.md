# Mechanisms of hospital activity mitigation

The 29 analyzed categories of potentially mitigable activity are divided into four broad groups based on the mechanism of mitigation. The first group, redirection and substitution, involves activity avoided by moving to community services. This includes admissions for frail older people, ambulatory care sensitive admissions, and medicines-related admissions. The second group, prevention, focuses on public health initiatives addressing smoking, obesity, and alcohol. The third group, relocation and efficiencies, primarily involves virtual wards. The fourth group, efficiencies, is distinct from the others as it does not aim to avoid admissions but rather to reduce the length of stay, thereby reducing bed days.
