# Collaborative data analysis in healthcare

The success of the day surgery analysis project was attributed to a methodology emphasizing close-knit collaboration between analysts, clinicians, and hospital managers. This approach involved linking massive amounts of data from diverse sources, including outpatient and inpatient records, lab tests, diagnoses, demography, community prescriptions, theaters, primary care, and hospital prescriptions. The project required a 12-month development period with regular meetings, allowing for constant feedback and the co-design of solutions.

This collaborative model helped overcome the common barrier of 'we know why it happens' by building trust and combining data expertise with ground-level operational insights. By involving stakeholders throughout the process, the analysis ensured that findings were not only statistically robust but also practically actionable. The integration of multidisciplinary perspectives facilitated the identification of nuanced factors, such as the impact of prescribing interface design and staffing patterns on discharge efficiency, demonstrating the value of sustained partnership in healthcare analytics.
