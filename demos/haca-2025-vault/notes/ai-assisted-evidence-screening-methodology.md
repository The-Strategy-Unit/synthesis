# AI-assisted evidence screening methodology

To address the challenge of identifying cost-saving interventions from hundreds of thousands of publications, an in-house NHS project developed an AI-assisted screening methodology. The process utilised Python and ChatGPT 4.0 (referred to in transcripts as 'Chad GPT 40' or 'GPT40') to screen over 5,000 reference papers. The choice of GPT 4.0 was based on its performance, validation results, and cost-effectiveness compared to newer versions. The methodology aims to replace slow, expensive, and resource-intensive manual reviews with a faster, scalable, and reliable evidence pipeline.

The screening pipeline consists of seven stages designed to filter papers based on specific criteria. Input data provided to the AI included study ID, publication year, title, and abstract. The model was tasked with determining inclusion or exclusion for each stage, outputting a binary decision, a reason limited to 20 characters, detected context keywords, and a confidence score between 0.9 and 1.0. The stages filter for English language and publication dates between 2019 and 2025, UK study applicability, and settings within NHS, health, social care, or community environments. It excludes pure laboratory schools and workplaces, as well as protocols and editorials.

Further stages require the presence of comparators, cost impact, and clinical outcomes. Crucially, the final stages assess alignment with the three major NHS strategic shifts and demonstrate cash-releasing benefits that reduce actual spend and budget, rather than just time or effort savings. Manual intervention is retained at the final stage to verify that selected papers focus on actual cost numbers.
