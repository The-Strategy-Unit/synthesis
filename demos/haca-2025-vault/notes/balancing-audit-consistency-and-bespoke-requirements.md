# Balancing Audit Consistency and Bespoke Requirements

A key challenge in dashboard development was balancing consistency across NATCAN audits with bespoke requirements for individual audits. Consistency requirements included maintaining a 'family feel' through uniform branding, layout, and navigation to ensure end-users could transfer skills across applications. It also involved standardising backend data formatting, such as healthcare provider names and their mapping to cancer alliances.
<!-- synthesis-claim:d53f9a84f652ab515a3f17430fbe1487d6591881fa8369df030fc6bea4047a77 -->

Conversely, bespoke requirements included audit-specific branding, metrics, and subgroup reporting. While most audits reported at the trust and cancer alliance levels, some, such as the National Ovarian Cancer Audit, required reporting at the gynaecological cancer system level. This tension necessitated a flexible architecture capable of supporting both standardised and unique elements. The use of R Markdown in visual editing mode facilitated broader engagement by enabling individuals without prior coding experience to edit text within the application, contributing to capacity building within the department.
