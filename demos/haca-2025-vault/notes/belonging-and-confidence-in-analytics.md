# Belonging and Confidence in Analytics

Confidence and belonging are described as structural factors in analytics rather than soft skills. Confident analysts are more likely to speak up, bring ideas, challenge issues, and remain in the profession longer. Community building is viewed as a driver of capability, strengthening the people who power analytical work. The future of analytics is characterised as emotional and cultural, with belonging shaping performance just as significantly as technical skills.

Initiatives like WIDAC aim to create safe environments where professionals feel seen and supported, thereby enhancing professional resilience and output. By fostering a sense of belonging, these initiatives seek to address the isolation and lack of confidence that often plague analysts, particularly women, in senior roles.
