# NATCAN Data Collection and Audit Structure

The National Cancer Audit Collaborating Centre (NATCAN) is an entity comprising ten national cancer audits in England and Wales. This portfolio includes six new cancer audits initiated in 2022 and four established audits that transitioned into NATCAN in 2023. The National Lung Cancer Audit is one of these ten audits.
<!-- synthesis-claim:d53f9a84f652ab515a3f17430fbe1487d6591881fa8369df030fc6bea4047a77 -->

NATCAN operates by utilising nationally mandated data flows from hospitals to the National Disease Registration Service in England and the Welsh Cancer Network in Public Health Wales. This methodology reduces the data burden on hospital staff, as clinical data is not collected directly from providers. Consequently, NHS organisations do not need to formally register for these audits or provide additional data beyond the standard registry submissions. The audits link cancer registry data to other datasets to provide comprehensive clinical information for every patient diagnosed with cancers covered by the programme.
