# Arthritis UK Lived Experience Survey methodology

The Arthritis UK Lived Experience Survey is the 2024 survey underlying Arthritis UK's *Left Waiting, Left Behind: The Reality of Living with Arthritis* report. It gathered quantitative data on the lived experience of people with arthritis across the four nations of the UK. The survey included 7,928 adults aged 18 and over, comprising both people with formal diagnoses and people without. Recruitment combined a commissioned YouGov panel of 5,144 respondents with targeted outreach through Arthritis UK's networks to 2,784 respondents.

To ensure robustness, the sample was weighted based on population prevalence of conditions, age, sex, and ethnicity distributions. The methodology specifically oversampled from underrepresented groups, including residents of Wales, Northern Ireland, and Scotland, individuals with less commonly reported conditions such as gout, and younger people. A steering group provided guidance, and results were reviewed by clinicians from various backgrounds. The survey covered topics including diagnosis, treatment, care, work, finances, and condition management. A significant limitation identified was the lack of robust data on ethnicity, preventing meaningful conclusions or breakdowns in that area.
