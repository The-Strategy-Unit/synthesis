# Linked NHS data analysis for suicide prevention

This methodological approach, conducted by Birmingham City Council, utilises linked NHS datasets to generate rapid, repeatable insights into suicide deaths. By moving beyond broad statistics, the analysis provides a holistic view of individuals' interactions with the health system prior to death. The project addresses the multifaceted nature of suicide by combining broad statistical trends with individual patient timelines, offering a comprehensive perspective on prevention opportunities.

The analysis relies on pseudonymised NHS numbers to link records from Aristotle, the Birmingham and Solihull Integrated Care Board's remote desktop platform. This dataset covers individuals who died in Birmingham and Solihull from 2020 onwards, integrating emergency department attendances, hospital admissions, emergency service calls, prescriptions, and mental health care contacts. The primary benefit is the ability to uncover demographic details, such as ethnicity, which may be missing from standard death registers, and to identify high-level overviews of health system interactions to pinpoint potential intervention points.
