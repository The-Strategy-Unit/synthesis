# Health system contact rates before suicide

Statistical findings from linked NHS data reveal the frequency of health system contact among individuals who died by suicide. An analysis of 620 registered suicides indicated that 78.5% of individuals had some form of contact with the health system through the linked datasets in the period leading up to their death. Specifically, 50% of these individuals had at least one physical health prescription in the 12 months prior to death.

Standardised mortality rates highlighted that age bands between 30 and 60 were significant. Among ethnic groups, only the white population showed statistically significant rates for males. These statistics are generated automatically via Qlik reports, allowing for repeatable analysis as data is pulled from SQL servers. This automated reporting facilitates the rapid identification of demographic patterns and healthcare utilisation trends associated with suicide risk.
