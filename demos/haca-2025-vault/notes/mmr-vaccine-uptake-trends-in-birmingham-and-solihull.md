# MMR vaccine uptake trends in Birmingham and Solihull

MMR vaccine coverage in Birmingham and Solihull has reached an all-time low, with MMR1 coverage at age five falling below 88% during the 2024–2025 period. This decline is occurring at a faster rate than the rest of England. While MMR2 coverage saw a slight increase in the most recent year, it remains significantly below the national average. These low uptake rates have contributed to increasingly serious measles outbreaks in the region, particularly in Birmingham, necessitating targeted interventions such as vaccine clinics and communications campaigns.
