# Analyst X Tool and Training Ecosystem

Analyst X is a tool available for download that allows users to populate their skills and benchmark themselves against the national peer-reviewed standard set by the competency framework. It is supported by a training ecosystem including fortnightly new adopter calls and weekly training sessions. These sessions cover tool usage and feature contributions from experienced users from organisations such as Arden & GEM, Mid and South Essex, and Imperial. The tool and associated events are listed on the Analyst X events calendar.

Analyst X is also described as one of the largest analytical communities, encompassing professionals from health, social care, local authorities, police, and education sectors. It serves as a platform for sharing resources, such as the national competence framework and case studies. The community hosts training sessions to support the adoption of the framework and professional development within the analytical world. For instance, NHS Arden & GEM Commissioning Support Unit, whose Business Intelligence service supports more than 30 health and care organisations, used the competency framework and Analyst X to understand its workforce structure. It employed a self-assessment tool for individuals and add-on tools for managers to aggregate individual profiles, allowing it to analyse skill composition, identify practitioner levels, and map strengths and gaps. This analysis helped structure discussions, identify areas for bespoke training, and support staff in career pathways.
