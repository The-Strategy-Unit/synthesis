# Integration of BI and Analysis Roles in NCF

Unlike the Government Analytical Services (GAD) framework, which separates Business Intelligence (BI) and analysis, the NHS Competency Framework merges these roles at the core level. This decision reflects the reality that such separation is not currently standardised in NHS job descriptions. The framework identifies core skills—such as numeracy, digital data skills, and data transformation—as common to both roles, particularly at junior levels. Specialisms are layered on top to reflect nuances and differentiate specific types of analysts. The framework is designed to adapt if the NHS moves towards standardised, separated job descriptions in the future.

The GAD framework is considered insufficient for the NHS because it is built for central government roles and must work across multiple departments, resulting in high-level, generic definitions. It does not reflect the full range of roles, additional skills, or specific requirements within the NHS. For example, GAD includes 'digital evaluator' but excludes broader evaluation skills used in the NHS, such as service evaluation. The NHS Competency Framework uses GAD as a 'bare bones' starting point, adding specific 'flesh' to make it more adaptable, targeted, and useful for training and recruitment within the health sector. There is an ongoing effort to produce standardised, Agenda for Change-approved, and panel-approved job descriptions based on the NCF, conducted with partners including Mid and South Essex and Digital Professions.
