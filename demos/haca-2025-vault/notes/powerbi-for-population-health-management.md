# PowerBI for Population Health Management

PowerBI is utilized to provide interactive filtering, map views, and underlying data tables to support population health management. The tool enables visualization of emerging concerns and practices at Primary Care Network (PCN), Sub-Commissioning Partnership (SCP), and practice levels. By displaying underlying tables, the system builds trust and transparency, reducing disputes when care packages are proposed. The dashboard includes ranking scores and banding for each practice, creating a priority list for action takers to target improvement programs effectively. Users can drill down into positive variations and other metrics to inform decision-making. Effective data modeling early in the development process is critical for unlocking flexibility in data presentation and visualization. Proper modeling reduces the need for extensive DAX coding and saves time when implementing new visualizations required for daily decision-making.
