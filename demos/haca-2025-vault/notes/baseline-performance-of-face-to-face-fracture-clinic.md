# Baseline Performance of Face-to-Face Fracture Clinic

Prior to the implementation of the virtual fracture clinic, the face-to-face fracture clinic at North Middlesex Hospital faced significant pressures in 2024. The clinic frequently booked more than 100 patients per day, averaging 72 patients per working day. Key performance issues included a 37.4% breach rate of the 72-hour target for seeing patients after presentation, with 66% of these breaches involving non-walking patients. The average wait for a plan review was 5.5 days.

Discharge rates for new attendances were 29%, and 9.8% of new patients required surgery. High 'did not attend' (DNA) rates were recorded at 23.5% for new face-to-face appointments and 30% for follow-ups. The clinic struggled with unpredictable demand due to multiple referral streams, including emergency department referrals, urgent elective cases, ward discharges, on-call referrals, and unscheduled walk-ins. Patient leaflets were often not provided or were only available in English, posing challenges in a population with high deprivation and significant language barriers.
