# Northumbria Healthcare endoscopy non-attendance prediction project

Northumbria Healthcare Foundation Trust initiated a project to address patient non-attendance (DNA) for endoscopy appointments, a problem costing the trust approximately £300,000 annually in lost revenue and additional costs. The trust serves a diverse geographic area including rural regions and urban areas near Newcastle, extending up to Scotland. The initiative aims to mitigate the financial impact and prevent patient health deterioration caused by missed appointments.

The project team, comprising Richard Bilsborough, Fabian Beigang, and Chris Wilcockson, developed a system to predict which patients are likely to miss appointments and implemented interventions to contact high-risk individuals. The core of the initiative involves a machine learning model trained on historical data to generate a DNA risk score, followed by a health navigation intervention where staff proactively contact flagged patients to address barriers such as transport, anxiety, or scheduling conflicts.
