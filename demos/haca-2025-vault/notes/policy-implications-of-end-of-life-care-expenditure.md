# Policy implications of end-of-life care expenditure

The findings on public expenditure for end-of-life care suggest an insufficient focus from policymakers on this demographic. The predominance of hospital care, particularly unplanned hospital care, as the largest driver of costs raises questions about resource allocation and the potential for shifting care to more comfortable settings. The study was cited in a Health Select Committee report, indicating its impact on policy discussions.

Future research is needed to compare UK spending with other countries, assess the effectiveness of the 10-year plan in changing the dial on hospital-based care, and explore how inequalities affect both the quality of care and the burden on public expenditure.
