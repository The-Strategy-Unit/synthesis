# Quantifying Vaccine Waste Reduction

Improved forecasting accuracy contributes to better prediction and reduced vaccine waste, but quantifying the exact financial impact remains challenging. Waste arises from factors such as pack sizes, for example, packs of 60 doses for flu vaccines, which create operational waste when demand does not match supply. While the forecasting system aims to align supply with demand more closely, isolating and measuring the specific monetary savings or linking forecast improvements directly to waste reduction figures is difficult. The complexity of operational variables makes it hard to attribute waste reduction solely to forecasting accuracy improvements.
