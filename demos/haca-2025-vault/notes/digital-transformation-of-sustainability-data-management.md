# Digital transformation of sustainability data management

The trust replaced a fragmented, manual data management system reliant on Excel and PDFs with a centralized dashboard using Power Apps and Power BI. Previously, sustainability data was scattered across multiple teams, with waste data coming from over 20 different supplier invoices in varying formats. The old carbon footprinting tool contained hundreds of linked formulas, was prone to errors, and was understood by only a few individuals, limiting reporting to quarterly trust-level summaries. The new system moves transformations, mappings, and calculations into Power BI, enabling real-time, secure access for senior leadership and operational managers. This shift improved data accessibility, reliability, and safety, reducing manual handling and allowing for instant output generation.

The development of the sustainability dashboard followed a structured iterative cycle: plan, design, develop, test, release, and feedback. During the planning stage, a clear vision was established and broken into manageable pieces. Workshops with operational users defined necessary features to support decision-making. Data quality was assessed, and schemas were aggregated to design data storage with minimal manual handling. Testing involved sharing early drafts with teams, such as the waste team, to gather feedback on visuals and calculations based on daily operational experience. This approach ensured the dashboard was technically accurate and genuinely useful, overcoming challenges related to inconsistent data formats and long-standing processes through dedicated resources and team upskilling.
