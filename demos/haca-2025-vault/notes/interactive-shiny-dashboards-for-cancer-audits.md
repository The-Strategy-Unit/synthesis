# Interactive Shiny Dashboards for Cancer Audits

NATCAN transitioned from static Excel reports to interactive Shiny dashboards to facilitate quarterly reporting and support quality improvement. The rollout occurred in two phases: Phase one, starting in July 2023, used the National Lung Cancer Audit as a pilot, transitioning from Excel to Shiny by April 2024. Phase two involved a NATCAN-wide deployment, with dashboards for all audits launched by April 2025. Sharing modular code across audits enabled 90% of dashboards to launch within three months.

The dashboards allow users to select a trust and indicator, displaying dynamic figures across three tabs: the selected trust, its cancer alliance, and a comparison of all trusts within that alliance. Information tabs provide details on indicator definitions, risk adjustment, control limits, and moving averages. Users can download figures as PNG files, aggregate data as CSV files, or generate trust-specific quarterly reports using parameterised R Markdown. This shift freed up analyst time for quality improvement initiatives and allowed for more frequent reporting, whereas previous reporting was annual.
