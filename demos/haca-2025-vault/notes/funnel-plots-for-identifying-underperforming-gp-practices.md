# Funnel plots for identifying underperforming GP practices

Funnel plots are used to identify GP practices with unexpectedly low vaccine uptake by visualising the proportion of patients receiving zero doses against the denominator of eligible children, such as those aged 2–4. Practices falling above control lines indicate underperformance relative to the mean and acceptable variation, signalling a need for intervention or support from Integrated Care Boards (ICBs). This method relies on data sources that provide both numerator (dose counts) and denominator (eligible population) data at the practice level, such as INFORM.
