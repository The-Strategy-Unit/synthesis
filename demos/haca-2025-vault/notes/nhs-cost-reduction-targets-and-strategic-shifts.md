# NHS cost reduction targets and strategic shifts

NHS providers are mandated to reduce costs by at least 1% in the years 2025 and 2026. This financial target necessitates the identification of interventions that deliver genuine cost savings, distinct from those that merely save time or effort. The requirement operates within a broader strategic framework defined by the NHS Long Term Plan, which outlines three major shifts guiding healthcare delivery: moving care from hospitals to communities, transitioning from analog to digital technologies, and prioritising the prevention of illness over treatment.

These strategic shifts serve as key criteria for evaluating potential interventions. Evidence supporting cost-saving measures aligned with these shifts is prioritised, reflecting the system's focus on structural efficiency and preventative care. The mandate creates pressure on Integrated Care Boards (ICBs) and NHS providers to locate and implement effective interventions amidst a vast and scattered body of published evidence.
