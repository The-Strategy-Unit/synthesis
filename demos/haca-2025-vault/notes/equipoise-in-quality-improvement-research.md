# Equipoise in Quality Improvement Research

Equipoise refers to the state of genuine uncertainty regarding the superiority of a quality improvement intervention over standard care or delayed facilitation. This uncertainty is essential for justifying the ethical basis of randomized trials, as it ensures that participants are not knowingly assigned to inferior treatments. In the context of the National Diabetes Audit, despite the long-standing provision of quality improvement collaboratives, there was equipoise about whether these interventions were actually more effective than not providing them.

This lack of definitive evidence supports the use of randomized comparisons to determine if such interventions lead to incremental improvements in patient care. By acknowledging this uncertainty, researchers can ethically test different strategies to identify those that genuinely enhance outcomes, rather than relying on assumptions about the effectiveness of established practices. Equipoise thus serves as a critical safeguard in quality improvement research, ensuring that interventions are rigorously evaluated before widespread implementation.
