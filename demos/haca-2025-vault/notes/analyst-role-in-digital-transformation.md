# Analyst Role in Digital Transformation

The role of analysts in digital transformation is characterised by a shift from established CIOs to analytical leaders driving data integration. This transition is compared to Bob Dylan replacing Joan Baez in the film 'I'm Not There', symbolising the need for analysts to step forward while CIOs step aside to allow data experts to figure out how to link data effectively. The key to successful digital transformation lies in data expertise and standards rather than software procurement. Analysts are positioned as central to the roadmap for data and digital convergence within Integrated Care Boards (ICBs), ensuring that technical decisions are informed by data integrity and usability. This perspective challenges the traditional reliance on management consultancy, arguing for stronger internal capability and rigorous data visualisation to reduce unnecessary external dependency.
