# Social Return on Investment Framework

Social Return on Investment (SROI) is a framework for measuring and accounting for social, economic, and environmental value. It is designed to assess the broader impact of activities, particularly in public health, where initiatives create lasting community benefits that are difficult to quantify using traditional methods. The framework promotes consistency and a shared language around concepts such as impact, returns, and value, while recognizing that what matters varies across people, situations, and cultures. Its goals include reducing inequality, preventing environmental harm, and improving well-being.

SROI moves beyond traditional metrics like cost-effectiveness and cost-benefit analysis by including the voices of all stakeholders and turning impact into a simple ratio, such as one pound of intervention creating three pounds of social value. It emphasizes that value is not just about money but about stories of change backed by data. The methodology is grounded in seven core principles derived from social accounting and cost-benefit analysis, which ensure consistency and credibility. These principles include involving stakeholders, understanding what changes, valuing what matters, avoiding overclaiming, and ensuring transparency and verification.
