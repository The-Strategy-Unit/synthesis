# Bridging technical and client language gaps

A common challenge in analytics is the language barrier between technically skilled analysts and clients who understand pathways, programs, and interventions but may not speak the same technical language. Overcoming this requires continuous engagement and conversation rather than expecting instant understanding. Analysts must learn the working practices and approaches of their stakeholders, bottoming out misunderstandings through dialogue. This process improves over time, making conversations more comfortable and effective as both parties align their expectations and terminology.

Analysts are positioned to support, enhance, and advocate for the use of analytical products, particularly when multiple users have diverse needs and approaches. By engaging from the beginning, analysts can understand how products are utilized, while users benefit from immediate responses from someone with analytical expertise. This role helps bridge the gap between technical delivery and user application, ensuring that products are effectively integrated into workflows and that communication between technical and non-technical stakeholders is improved. This bridging function is essential for ensuring that analytical outputs are not just delivered, but actually used to inform decision-making.
