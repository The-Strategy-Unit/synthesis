# Stakeholder Engagement and Strategic Planning

Identifying and understanding the users of the model is a complex and ongoing challenge. The perspectives of users multiply as the project progresses, making it difficult to define a single user profile. Structured ways of thinking about these diverse user needs have been identified as a major benefit and a key area for continued development. This understanding is critical for the model's utility and acceptance.

A key challenge in data science teams is separating operational work from strategic work. Operational demands, such as immediate requests to check parameter changes for meetings, can distract from long-term strategic goals like building future-facing models and features. To mitigate this, it is advised to use Agile and Scrum methodologies to facilitate better conversations and maintain a clear roadmap. This helps stakeholders understand the trade-offs of diverting resources to operational tasks. Advocating for proper working conditions and resisting the pressure for immediate results is essential for sustainable progress. Current modelling efforts are expanding to national and regional levels to analyze acute activity across whole regions, with interest in developing a 'bring your own data' model and exploring Federated Data Platforms (FDP) to address information governance problems.
