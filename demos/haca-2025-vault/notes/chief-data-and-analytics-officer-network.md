# Chief Data and Analytics Officer Network

The Chief Data and Analytics Officer Network is a professional body established to support senior analytical professionals, filling a gap alongside existing organisations such as AphA, Fed IP, the Strategy Unit, and the Health Economics Unit. The network aims to raise the profile of analysts and support younger analytical leaders through various channels, including Teams, an annual event, a monthly webinar series, and an active presence on the AphA website and LinkedIn. The national chair views the network as a mechanism to ensure that data expertise informs strategic decisions rather than being sidelined by clinicians or Chief Information Officers (CIOs) who may lack specific data integration knowledge.
