# Qualitative evaluation of service impact

Following the quantitative analysis of the Right Care Right Person initiative, the next steps for the project involve qualitative evaluation to provide a more rounded view of the initiative's impact. This includes setting up focus groups and conducting interviews with partners and stakeholders across the wider system. The goal is to understand perspectives that quantitative numbers may miss, such as localized pressures within specific parts of services that do not show up in overall demand figures. This approach aims to deepen the understanding of how one organization's policy affects interconnected parts of the wider system.
