# Multidisciplinary Prioritization in Complex Projects

In complex projects with limited resources and high demand, standard prioritization criteria may fail due to political dynamics and evolving requirements. A multidisciplinary team of 'makers' was established to handle prioritization by integrating multiple factors: political context, credibility levels, interpersonal dynamics, and available skill sets.
<!-- synthesis-claim:a8eab1152ce43a4f7b61c07e957e4e57fc4e55508cca1575b3a6e913d30aa33a -->

This approach required constant flexibility, as priorities shifted weekly based on immediate threats to credibility or resource availability. The process involved saying 'no' to non-critical requests to maintain focus on essential deliverables, ensuring the project remained on track despite chaotic conditions. This method was later adopted for broader data science strategy work.
