# Stakeholder Engagement in Health Data

Gaining buy-in for data-driven tools involves presenting to influential bodies such as local medical councils and members of parliament to build confidence among GP practices. Transparency is key; showing raw data tables before processing helps prevent pushback, as the data is difficult to deny. Benchmarking practices against local and national peers provides a shared evidence base for early supportive conversations. This approach frees up analyst time from spreadsheet maintenance, allowing for more interpretation and value-added activities, such as directly contacting practices with appropriate transformation packs. The tool aids in targeting specific practices for bespoke care packages and improvement initiatives by allowing users to marry other metrics, such as deprivation, to rerank practices. It also includes 'soft intel' columns to identify existing programs, helping to reframe resources and avoid duplication.
