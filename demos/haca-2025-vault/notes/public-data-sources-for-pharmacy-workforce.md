# Public Data Sources for Pharmacy Workforce

Three specific publicly available datasets are identified for building community pharmacy workforce models from scratch, allowing external users to access necessary data without internal NHS employee credentials. The first is the GPhC register data. The second is the Community Pharmacy Contractual Framework (CPCF) survey data, which has been mandated by regulation since October 2022. The third is the NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA) consolidated format list, accessible via API or as Excel/CSV downloads.
<!-- synthesis-claim:745847cc6c3f79e0a10b45acead91a1380e28da9e4f6fa61470a8171e806bc68 -->

These sources provide the foundational data required for workforce analysis. The NHSBSA data, in particular, is often split by quarter and year, requiring users to identify relevant resource IDs to retrieve specific variables such as total pharmacist numbers or average weekly opening hours. This data can then be encapsulated in variables for further calculation or function input.
