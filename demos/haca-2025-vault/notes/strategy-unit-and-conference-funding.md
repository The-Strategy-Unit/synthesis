# Strategy Unit and Conference Funding

The Strategy Unit is an NHS team responsible for organising HACA. In 2025, staff organised the event outside their regular duties. The conference operates without commercial sponsorship to preserve its non-commercial character. Due to the absence of NHS funding for the 2025 iteration, the event relied on external support to avoid cancellation. The Health Foundation provided the platform, and HDR UK contributed funding. Organisers emphasise the importance of attendee feedback and advocacy to ensure the conference's continued existence as a stable part of the analytical ecosystem.
