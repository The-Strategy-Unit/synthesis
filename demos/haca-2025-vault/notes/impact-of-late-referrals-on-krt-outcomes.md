# Impact of late referrals on KRT outcomes

Late referrals into the kidney replacement therapy system, often occurring via emergency routes due to undetected chronic kidney disease, are associated with worse patient outcomes. The modeling framework identifies that patients entering the system late have a higher probability of adverse events compared to those identified earlier. Reducing late referrals is considered a key intervention to improve outcomes and manage system capacity. This insight supports the broader goal of using simulation to predict the effects of interventions on this complex system, allowing for the exploration of feedback interactions and interconnectedness that are difficult to assess through traditional planning methods.
