# NHS waiting list analysis objectives

The Networked Data Lab project established four primary objectives for analysing NHS waiting lists, developed in collaboration with the Health Foundation and applied across all participating labs. The first objective involved describing wait lengths for patients awaiting elective care. The second examined the reasons for removal from the waiting list. The third objective analysed primary and secondary care healthcare usage before and after the waiting period. The fourth addressed a causal question, assessing the impact of longer waiting periods on healthcare usage and associated costs.

These objectives were designed to provide a comprehensive view of the waiting list experience, moving beyond simple duration metrics to understand the broader healthcare journey and potential consequences of delays. The analysis aimed to inform policy and improvement areas by identifying patterns in care usage and the effectiveness of current waiting list management.
