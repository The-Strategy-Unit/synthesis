# Cost drivers in lower limb surgery

Analysis of surgical expenditures identifies theater staff time as the most significant cost driver, exceeding £1,300 per hour for staff alone, excluding overheads. In knee surgeries, nearly 90% of costs stemmed from theater staff, non-pay items such as swabs, and ward expenses. Ward costs alone accounted for approximately £22,000 in the knee dataset, while implants and loan kits contributed about £20,000.

For hip surgeries, theater staff costs reached around £36,000, with ward costs representing 20% of total expenditure due to longer inpatient stays. Implants constituted 10.2% of hip surgery costs, compared to 5.8% for knees and less than 1.5% for foot and ankle cases. Drugs, imaging, and pathology collectively made up under 5% of total costs. Foot and ankle surgery operates on a leaner model, with theater staff as the largest expense at just over £19,000, driven by faster turnover and efficient list management. Crucially, every foot and ankle case in the study was performed as a day procedure, eliminating inpatient day costs entirely.
