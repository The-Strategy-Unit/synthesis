# NHS Data Infrastructure and Documentation

Adequate technical documentation is identified as the third, often overlooked pillar of data infrastructure, alongside safe and secure data access. Documentation should be created by predecessors and organised by professionals whose primary role is to produce high-quality technical documentation. It is considered infrastructure itself, enabling analysts to quickly access the data they need without relying on tacit knowledge held by individuals.

The lack of such infrastructure contributes significantly to the loss of institutional knowledge. When individual analysts leave or complete specific tasks, moments of insight are not captured in reusable systems. Proper documentation ensures that analytical work is reproducible and that the organisation retains the capacity to utilise its data assets effectively, regardless of staff turnover.
