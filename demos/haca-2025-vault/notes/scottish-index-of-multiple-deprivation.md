# Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation

The Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation (SIMD) is a statistical tool used to measure deprivation in Scotland. The 2016 publication identified Ferguslie Park in Paisley as the most deprived area, a status it had held since the index began. Analysis revealed that low income and unemployment were the primary drivers of deprivation, contradicting local council narratives that focused on housing stock investment. By 2020, Ferguslie Park was no longer ranked as the most deprived area.

The development of SIMD involved collaboration with local charities to better describe local problems and ensure communities could utilize the data. This included integrating testimonies about the impact of poverty on mental health and social isolation. To challenge the opacity of high-level indices, the 2016 SIMD was republished as a 12-page pamphlet rather than a 140-page report, incorporating personal stories. This format aimed to make leaders uncomfortable with the human cost of statistics, prompting action rather than allowing problems to be hidden within large, technical publications.
