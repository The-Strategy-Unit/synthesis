# Excess Impacts dashboard for ED monitoring

The 'Excess Impacts' dashboard is a dynamic tool designed to communicate the results of emergency department (ED) modeling to executive-level managers and planners. It refreshes weekly with live data to show operational impacts, featuring a 'counterfactual' or 'what-if' analysis structure. The dashboard displays standard headlines of recent weekly events alongside bar charts comparing two scenarios: the estimated impacts based on actual data and a hypothetical scenario where boarding times are kept under one hour and boarding queues remain under 25% of ED capacity.

This comparison allows users to visualize potential operational changes and improvements, such as reductions in ambulance response times, if specific levers were pulled. The tool aims to simplify complex modeling results for non-technical stakeholders and facilitate meaningful operational changes. The reception of the counterfactual analysis among clinicians and managers has been largely positive, despite initial concerns. Previously, there was risk aversion among higher-up managers regarding the reporting of issues like excess deaths, fearing negative media attention. However, the simplicity and clarity of the dashboard have been well-received. Some stakeholders have used the tool to advocate for necessary changes, leveraging the data to highlight ongoing problems. The dashboard includes interactive elements, such as dials, allowing users to tweak scenarios and explore different operational outcomes.
