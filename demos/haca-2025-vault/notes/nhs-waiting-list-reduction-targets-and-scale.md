# NHS waiting list reduction targets and scale

The size of the NHS waiting list has increased since approximately 2011, with a significant surge occurring after December 2019 due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Prior to this surge, the list was already significantly off target. Current government priorities involve reducing the waiting list to target levels by the end of the next parliament, estimated to end in 2029. This reduction requires removing approximately one million people from the waiting list annually. Estimates suggest the national waiting list size needs to be roughly half its current size to meet targets, with target levels previously cited between 2.4 million and 3.6 million.
