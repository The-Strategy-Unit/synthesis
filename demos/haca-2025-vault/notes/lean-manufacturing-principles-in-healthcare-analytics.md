# Lean manufacturing principles in healthcare analytics

Lean manufacturing principles, particularly those derived from Japanese manufacturing culture, are applied to healthcare analytics to address cultural, political, and operational challenges. Tom Smith distinguishes between three concepts: waste, unevenness (variation), and overburden, referencing the 'seven wastes' or 'eight wastes' of lean methodology. While technical problems often resolve through engineering, Smith notes that his role frequently involves addressing non-technical issues. An example from his manufacturing career involved addressing late deliveries from a gearbox supplier by videoing processes and reducing human contact time from 40 hours to 12 hours without capital investment, resulting in improved quality and timeliness. These lean principles are viewed as formative tools for problem-solving in the NHS, emphasizing process improvement and waste elimination.
