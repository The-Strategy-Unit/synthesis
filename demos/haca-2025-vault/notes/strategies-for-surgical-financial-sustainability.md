# Strategies for surgical financial sustainability

To address financial losses in complex lower limb surgery, five recommendations are proposed to improve sustainability. First, increase cases per day by adding short, efficient cases to underused lists to boost revenue without significantly increasing staffing costs, prioritizing on-time starts and minimal turnaround. Second, implement enhanced recovery pathways, including early mobilization and optimized analgesia, to reduce length of stay, particularly for hip and knee procedures, thereby cutting ward costs.

Third, improve discharge planning by identifying social and clinical barriers pre-operatively and using multidisciplinary team-led discharge from day one to reduce ward costs per day. Fourth, secure fair tariff agreements that reflect true local costs, especially for high-complexity and high-implant-cost cases. Fifth, benchmark against elective hub theaters to identify transferable models, such as scheduling strategies and case mix efficiencies, from high-performing hubs. These strategies target high-impact cost areas rather than low-impact categories like drugs or imaging.
