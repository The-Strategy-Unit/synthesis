# Asthma outcome improvement in Dorset

Dorset historically exhibited higher asthma mortality rates than the England average and other Integrated Care Boards (ICBs) in the southwest for approximately 15 years. To address this, clinicians collaborated with business intelligence partners to utilise the DICE database for population health management. The strategy involved using data to inform leadership, strategy, and priorities, supported by a network of 18 primary care networks, each with a respiratory champion (practice nurse). This approach facilitated PDSA cycles and workforce engagement. Between 2022 and 2025, Dorset saw significant improvements in reducing the overuse of short-acting beta-2 agonists (SABAs), becoming one of the best-performing regions in England for this metric, alongside Gloucestershire. The improvement is attributed to the combination of data insights, clinical leadership, and workforce access.
