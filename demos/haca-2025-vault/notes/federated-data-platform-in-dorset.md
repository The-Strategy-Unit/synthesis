# Federated Data Platform in Dorset

The federated data and intelligence platform in Dorset securely integrates data from various sources, including two acute trusts, community healthcare providers, primary care GP practices, local authorities, and the ambulance service. This integration breaks down organizational boundaries, allowing stakeholders to see where individuals interact with multiple parts of the health and social care system. By combining traditional NHS datasets with local authority data, such as Mosaic Experian datasets for financial vulnerability risks and adult social care interactions, the platform supports a comprehensive assessment of individual risk and location.

The platform features re-identification functionality, enabling authorized users to filter and analyze populations through tools like a single-page PowerBI report. This allows local authority areas to target specific populations and primary care providers to re-identify individuals within their GP surgeries for targeted outreach. The infrastructure supports the identification of vulnerable populations, analysis of conditions and interventions, and monitoring of activity and impact, facilitating a shift towards prevention and community-based care.
