# Dorset Integrated Care System Collaborative Model

The Dorset Integrated Care System implemented a collaborative approach involving GPs, social prescribers, adult social care, voluntary and community sector partners, acute trusts, local authorities, the GP alliance, Wessex Water, and local housing associations. A key mechanism was a fortnightly, half-hourly meeting without a fixed agenda, allowing partners to share observations and identify opportunities for joint action. This structure helped break down organizational boundaries and facilitated organic connections, such as charities inviting individuals to weekly forums. The project utilized the DICE system's re-identification functionality to engage across system partners and increase awareness of available spaces, such as libraries serving as warm spaces during winter.

Proactive outreach was a key outcome, targeting both individuals and specific communities. Partners reported seeing firsthand instances where support was provided sooner, preventing falls or other crises, rather than waiting for health service repercussions. The outreach included offering energy efficiency booklets to help heat-proof homes and collaborating with Dorset Police Service to include messaging around preventing fraud. The evaluation showed a small reduction in the average number of primary care visits for the cohort involved, though it did not achieve all four initial outcomes. The success was attributed to collaborative efforts and the ability to make early interventions, with plans to continue monitoring long-term impacts.
