# Limitations of A&E predictive modelling

Predictive modelling of A&E waiting times faced several inherent limitations. The data was heavily skewed, resulting in high prediction errors even with sophisticated models. Key predictive variables, such as baseline virus activity on a given day and GP appointment availability, were not routinely available, potentially limiting model accuracy. The study was also constrained by time, capacity, storage, and processing limitations, which prevented the exploration of additional approaches like deep learning or extensive fine-tuning.

Attempts at fine-tuning were abandoned due to the size of the model and prolonged processing times. The complexity of hospital data meant that not all variability could be explained by any single model. Furthermore, there was a noted challenge in communicating complex, nuanced findings to senior managers, who often prefer standard Business Intelligence (BI) dashboard outputs over detailed interpretative analysis. This highlights a gap between advanced analytical capabilities and organisational readiness to utilise nuanced insights.
