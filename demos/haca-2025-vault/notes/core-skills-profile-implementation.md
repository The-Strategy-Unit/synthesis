# Core Skills Profile Implementation

A method for defining team capabilities based on frameworks such as the National Competency Framework and analytical skills families. In the context of the NHS England team, this involved setting specific proficiency targets, such as ensuring 30% of the team achieved proficiency in GIS or evaluation statistics, and 100% at grade level in core statistical skills.

Individual SMART objectives are tailored to this profile, ensuring that skill development is distributed across the team. This approach prevents the requirement for universal expertise in every domain, allowing analysts to develop deep skills in specific areas while maintaining baseline competence in core methods. The profile serves as a benchmark for assessing current capabilities and planning future development.
