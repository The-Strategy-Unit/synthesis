# Critique of Whole Population Risk Stratification

Despite policy enthusiasm, whole population risk stratification is presented as an ineffective method for targeting care. This perspective is supported by a critical assessment of the evidence produced by Jess Small, which has been published recently but is reportedly widely disregarded by policymakers. Steven Wyatt is scheduled to discuss this observation in a keynote session, highlighting the disconnect between available evidence and current policy implementation regarding risk stratification methods.
