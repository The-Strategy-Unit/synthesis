# Policy recommendations for arthritis care

A report on arthritis management outlines five key policy recommendations aimed at improving care and reducing inequalities. First, recognizing arthritis and MSK conditions as a major public health issue. Second, ensuring equal access to personalized treatment and care regardless of geographic location. Third, improving the quality and availability of data related to arthritis. Fourth, improving and embedding training for all frontline healthcare professionals to enhance diagnosis and support. Fifth, protecting individuals from being pushed into poverty and hardship due to their condition.

These interventions are intended to result in higher quality services, reduced inequalities, improved diagnosis, and greater visibility of arthritis and MSK health through better data. The policy recommendations were developed by an internal policy team and represent a continuation of previous organizational calls to action, now supported by new data from the survey.
