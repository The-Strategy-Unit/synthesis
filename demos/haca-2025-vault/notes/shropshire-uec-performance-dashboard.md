# Shropshire UEC Performance Dashboard

The Shropshire Urgent and Emergency Care (UEC) Performance Dashboard is a system-wide intelligence tool developed by the Shropshire, Telford and Wrekin Integrated Care Board (ICB) and its Strategic Data Warehouse (SDW) team. Designed to address growing pressure on UEC services, the dashboard provides insights spanning primary care, hospital urgent care, and community-based care. It integrates data from pre-hospital services, emergency department usage, inpatient care, and discharge processes. The tool is updated daily, typically around midday, and utilizes unvalidated data feeds for timeliness, complemented by other SDW dashboards using published accountable metrics. The dashboard aims to enable operational frontline teams and service leaders to respond quickly to emerging pressures by providing visibility of performance against targets, plans, and prior intervals.
