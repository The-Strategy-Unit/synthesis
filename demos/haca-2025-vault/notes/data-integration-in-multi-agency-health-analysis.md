# Data integration in multi-agency health analysis

Creating a dashboard to link police, county council, and healthcare data for the Suffolk evaluation presented significant technical and administrative challenges. Key difficulties included managing population health management systems split across two different areas with incompatible systems, requiring complex data extraction. The analysis utilized Suffolk Constabulary data as the primary source, tracking referrals via a Power BI dashboard. This was matched against data from the Suffolk population health management system (Axiom), provided by the NHS North of England Commissioning Support Unit (NECS), and children and adult social care data from Suffolk County Council.

The study covered areas spanning two Integrated Care Systems, requiring data collection from two separate population health management systems. Population health management data resided in a secure data environment, necessitating aggregation within that environment and extraction via a PowerBI gateway. Securing permissions and demonstrating the purpose of the data use to stakeholders was a major hurdle, contributing to a two-year delay between the initiative's inception and the presentation of results. The project avoided linking identifiable data to reduce complexity, focusing instead on aggregated metrics.
