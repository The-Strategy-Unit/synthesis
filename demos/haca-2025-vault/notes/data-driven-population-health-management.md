# Data-driven population health management

Effective population health management requires more than just data; it necessitates a combination of data insights, strong leadership, strategic prioritisation, and an engaged workforce. In the context of Dorset's asthma initiative, data from the DICE platform was used to identify variations, trends, and associations, such as inhaler use versus outcomes. This information informed business cases for resource allocation and guided respiratory champions in primary care networks to take targeted actions. The process involves continuous feedback loops, such as PDSA cycles, to evaluate the effectiveness of interventions and adjust strategies accordingly.
