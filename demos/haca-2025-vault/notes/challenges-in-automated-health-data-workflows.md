# Challenges in automated health data workflows

Persistent issues are encountered even after implementing automated data pipelines for health reporting. Real-world data quality issues such as backdated entries, changes in service names, and occasional typos still occur. Current solutions involve manual checks before generating reports to ensure accuracy. The workflow design intentionally retains a manual 'render' step for quality assurance, acknowledging that full automation at any cost is not the goal.

Instead, a balance is sought between time-saving automation and necessary human oversight for judgment-critical tasks. Future improvements may include automated data freshness checks and alert functions to notify staff of unusual data patterns. These challenges highlight the importance of maintaining human-in-the-loop processes to handle exceptions and ensure the reliability of automated health data systems.
