# Agile adaptation in data science

Agile methodologies are viewed as essential for data science but require adaptation rather than direct porting from digital product development. While clients may request clear, defined outcomes in a 'waterfall' fashion, their actual needs often evolve, making a flexible approach more effective for customer satisfaction. Agility is described as a mindset and mode of practice embedded in code, team management, and rituals, rather than just a set of empty procedures.

However, standard agile methods do not fit neatly into data science workflows, particularly when dealing with complex simulations or large-scale models. The community is encouraged to develop its own tailored approaches to agility. This is especially relevant given that data science is entering a 'max power stage' with the ability to process millions of rows of data quickly using modern tools and AI-powered capabilities. Despite this technical speed, there is a risk of 'reinventing the wheel' or producing outputs that do not align with user needs if the agile process is not properly adapted to the unique challenges of data science.
