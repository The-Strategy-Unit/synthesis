# Iterative dashboard development methodology

The development of the sustainability dashboard followed a structured iterative cycle: plan, design, develop, test, release, and feedback. During the planning stage, a clear vision was established and broken into manageable pieces. Workshops with operational users defined necessary features to support decision-making. Data quality was assessed, and schemas were aggregated to design data storage with minimal manual handling. Testing involved sharing early drafts with teams, such as the waste team, to gather feedback on visuals and calculations based on daily operational experience. This approach ensured the dashboard was technically accurate and genuinely useful, overcoming challenges related to inconsistent data formats and long-standing processes through dedicated resources and team upskilling.
