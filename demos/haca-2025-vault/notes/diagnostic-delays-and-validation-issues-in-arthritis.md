# Diagnostic delays and validation issues in arthritis

Obtaining a diagnosis for arthritis, particularly inflammatory forms, was reported as a prolonged and difficult process. Four in 10 respondents felt the diagnostic process took too long, which is critical because early diagnosis prevents condition progression. Nearly half felt their symptoms were not taken seriously or were underplayed by healthcare professionals, an experience more common among those in lower social grades.

Six in 10 people stated that a diagnosis did not validate their experience, rising to 70% for those with autoimmune inflammatory conditions. Two in three reported that waiting for treatment impacted their mental health somewhat or a great deal, with higher impacts observed in lower social grades. Respondents suggested that earlier diagnosis and treatment could have lessened the impact on both themselves and the NHS. These diagnostic and validation challenges contribute to the broader barriers in accessing effective care.
