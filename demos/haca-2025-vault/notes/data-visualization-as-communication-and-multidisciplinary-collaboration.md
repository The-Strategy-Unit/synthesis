# Data visualization as communication and multidisciplinary collaboration

Effective data visualization is described as being more about 'data and slight visualization' rather than pure visualization, highlighting a gap in current practices regarding communication standards. Key considerations often pushed aside include responsibility, branding, color usage, accessibility, and neurodiversity. Adopting multidisciplinary teams (MDTs) is proposed as a solution to integrate roles such as user researchers, content developers, and graphic designers. This approach emphasizes collaboration over siloed work, ensuring that visualizations consider broader organizational impact. To improve decision-making in meetings, visual and graphical communication, such as flowcharts and diagrams, is proposed as the norm to facilitate agreement or clarify disagreements. Ensuring every meeting room has functional whiteboards and pens is suggested as a practical enabler for visual collaboration.
