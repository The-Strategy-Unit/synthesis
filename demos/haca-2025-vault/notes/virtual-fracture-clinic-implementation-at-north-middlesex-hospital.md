# Virtual Fracture Clinic Implementation at North Middlesex Hospital

A virtual fracture clinic (VFC) was implemented at North Middlesex Hospital, part of the North Central London integrated care system, to address overcrowding and compliance issues in the face-to-face outpatient orthopedic clinic. The service operated from February to March 2024, processing 484 referrals over 5.2 weeks, with an average of 18 referrals per day. The VFC was designed for acute orthopedic injuries manageable non-operatively, aiming to reduce unnecessary face-to-face attendance, improve clinic flow, and adhere to British Orthopaedic Association (BOA) standards.

The workflow utilised an integrated care system called Careflow. Admin staff booked patients onto a clinic list where histories and imaging were available. A consultant and an Advanced Practitioner (A&P) reviewed these details remotely, proposing a plan and contacting patients via telephone. Following the consultation, a letter was dictated and sent to both the General Practitioner and the patient. Decisions were communicated within 72 hours, resulting in either a clear discharge plan or escalation to a face-to-face review. Patient leaflets and exercise sheets were provided at the time of referral to support self-management.
