# Unstructured data analysis in social care

The application of large language models and natural language processing to ingest and explore unstructured data sources, such as case notes and inspection reports. This capability aims to build a richer, more nuanced picture of adult social care beyond structured metrics. It represents a potential future function of the proposed data infrastructure to handle complex, non-tabular information.
<!-- synthesis-claim:e0bf58afc351024893434f8c2945370b10ec25384c36dcde44b1c4a27e0f61d9 -->

By addressing the 'silence' in data regarding lived experience, this approach seeks to reveal insights that traditional quantitative methods may miss. Integrating unstructured data analysis with existing structured data systems can enhance the understanding of individual care journeys and support more informed decision-making in adult social care.
