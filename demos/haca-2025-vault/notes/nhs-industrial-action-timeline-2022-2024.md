# NHS Industrial Action Timeline 2022-2024

Between December 2022 and July 2024, the National Health Service (NHS) in England experienced approximately 20 months of industrial action. This period comprised 27 strikes covering a total of 78 days. The action was primarily driven by high inflation and stagnant wages during the cost of living crisis.
<!-- synthesis-claim:e40025cc033d58ebc7da13c61995ba03b5fdae23b6fc51313fc734870f34a5b2 -->

The strikes involved various clinical groups. Early actions were led by nurses and paramedics, with settlements reached in spring 2023. Resident doctors (formerly known as junior doctors) began striking in June 2023 and continued their action until July 2024. Consultants also engaged in industrial action in mid-2023. On several occasions, strikes by resident doctors and consultants coincided, creating periods of simultaneous staffing shortages across different levels of the medical hierarchy.
