# Limitations of UK end-of-life cost data

The estimation of public expenditure on end-of-life care in the UK faces several data limitations. There are significant gaps in data for community services and social care, with a lack of access to granular datasets. The study could not link health, social care, and social security data directly to deaths, relying instead on published literature. There is no dedicated source of unit cost data specific to end-of-life care, meaning identified cost data was not always specific to this context.

Additionally, while nation-specific cost data was available for England, Scotland, and Northern Ireland, activity data was incomplete across all four nations, requiring the use of proxy data where gaps existed. These limitations highlight areas for further research, including understanding inequalities in care location and the impact of deprivation on end-of-life care experiences.
