# Propositional Approach to NHS Data Progress

Progress in NHS data analytics requires a propositional approach rather than merely critiquing current practices or stating how the world should be. Individuals and organisations should identify specific pathways and new projects that deliver material value in the short term while laying the groundwork for future efficiency. This includes creating online onboarding materials, applying for funding to generalise tools, building websites that foster communities of practice, and offering courses to build capacity.

Progress is built by those who can push through resistance and drive cautious controversy to overcome bad practice while promoting good practice. The initial response to proposals is often negative, but persistent, constructive action is necessary to effect change. This method focuses on actionable steps that demonstrate value, thereby building momentum for broader systemic improvements in data usage and infrastructure.
