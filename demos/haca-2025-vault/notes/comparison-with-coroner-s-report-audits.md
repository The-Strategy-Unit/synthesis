# Comparison with coroner's report audits

The administrative data approach is contrasted with traditional suicide audits that rely on coroner's reports. Coroner's narrative reports can be difficult to standardise and extract consistent information from, as they may vary significantly in length and detail. The administrative data method offers a faster, more rapid pace of analysis using routinely collected datasets, allowing for broader statistical insights and repeatable reporting.

However, the administrative data method does not replace the depth of narrative understanding found in coroner reports. While it provides a 'best of both worlds' perspective by combining broad trends with individual timelines, the insights are constrained by data quality. Incorporating more complex datasets, such as social and economic indicators, could improve the understanding of individual conditions and risk factors, bridging the gap between quantitative administrative data and qualitative narrative evidence.
