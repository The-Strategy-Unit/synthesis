# Women in Data and Analytics Conference (WIDAC)

WIDAC is a conference and community initiative established by Louise Killen, Analytics Manager at Northumbria Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust and chair of the conference. The initiative was created to address the underrepresentation of women in senior analytical roles within the National Health Service (NHS), a sector where less than 30% of senior-grade analysts are women. Unlike typical technical events, WIDAC focuses on the human aspects of the profession, emphasising belonging, visibility, confidence, and career growth.

The inaugural event took place in the North East of England and featured keynote speakers including Dr Birju Bartoli, Chief Executive of Northumbria Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust, and Samantha Allen, then Chief Executive of NHS North East and North Cumbria Integrated Care Board. The conference is free to attend and open to all genders, including allies, with the aim of replicating its model in other regions. Post-event feedback highlighted themes of reduced isolation, increased confidence, and a supportive atmosphere among attendees.
