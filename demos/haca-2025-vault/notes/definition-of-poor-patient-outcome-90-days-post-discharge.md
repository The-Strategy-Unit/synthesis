# Definition of poor patient outcome 90 days post-discharge

In this analysis, a 'poor outcome' within 90 days of hospital discharge is defined by three specific criteria: readmission to hospital (emergency admissions only), starting a new long-term residential service in adult social care, or death. This definition was selected to align with the Adult Social Care Framework (ASCOF) statistics produced by the Department of Health and Social Care. The outcome serves as the binary target variable for supervised learning models predicting discharge success.
