# Psychological Safety in Data Analytics Teams

Psychological safety and neurodiversity awareness are emphasized as crucial components of the Woodyard Model, particularly in its implementation at Kent and Medway ICB. Creating a psychologically safe environment is considered essential for reducing wasted effort, as the model relies on people feeling able to bring their full selves and best thinking to the 'bench.' When team members can surface risks early, be honest about uncertainty, and push back when something doesn't fit the model without fear of stepping on toes, the team gels better and produces higher-quality work.

This human-centric approach is viewed as equally important as technical improvements. It supports a culture where analysts can contribute effectively, balancing speed with quality and ensuring that risks are identified and addressed promptly. The emphasis on psychological safety helps to foster collaboration and innovation within data teams, contributing to the overall success of structured data operating models.
