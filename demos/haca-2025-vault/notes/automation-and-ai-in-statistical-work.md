# Automation and AI in Statistical Work

In the context of official statistics, automation and artificial intelligence are viewed not as threats but as tools to streamline repetitive tasks. The goal is to reduce the time statisticians spend on creating analysis so they can focus on higher-value activities, such as engaging with decision-makers and telling compelling stories from the data. This shift allows for a greater emphasis on communicating uncertainty and building trust in official statistics, rather than merely producing numbers.

The adoption of these technologies is seen as a means to an end, enabling a more strategic and impactful use of statistical expertise. However, effective implementation requires high-quality underlying data. Without sorted, reliable data infrastructure, new tools may create more problems than value. Therefore, the focus remains on using automation to free up capacity for narrative-driven analysis and community engagement, ensuring that statistical work leads to tangible policy improvements.
