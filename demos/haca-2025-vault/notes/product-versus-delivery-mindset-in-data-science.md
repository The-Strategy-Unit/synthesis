# Product versus delivery mindset in data science

In data science and analytics, a critical distinction exists between the 'product mindset' and the 'delivery mindset'. The product mindset focuses on the 'why' and 'what' of a solution, prioritizing user needs, business context, and the rationale behind the work. It involves understanding what users can understand and designing outputs that make sense to them. In contrast, the delivery mindset concerns the 'how', managing technical execution, computations, data locations, and project timelines.

Combining these roles is often problematic because they require different skill sets and mindsets. When product and delivery responsibilities are merged, teams risk producing technically sophisticated outputs that fail to address underlying user needs or solve the correct problem. Separating these functions allows each discipline to receive appropriate respect and clarity, preventing the common pitfall of analysts being asked for specific outputs without first establishing the fundamental question or user context. This separation is particularly important in data science, where these disciplines are often incorrectly modeled together, leading to confusion in career paths and responsibilities.
