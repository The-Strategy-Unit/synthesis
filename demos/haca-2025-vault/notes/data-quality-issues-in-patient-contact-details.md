# Data quality issues in patient contact details

Analysis of non-attendance rates revealed a critical issue with patient contact data. While successfully contacted high-risk patients had a low non-attendance rate of 4.9%, those who could not be reached exhibited a rate of 40.5%. This disparity suggests that the high non-attendance among uncontacted patients is not merely due to patient reluctance but likely stems from inaccurate or outdated phone numbers and contact details. Consequently, future quality improvement projects are planned to focus on enhancing data quality to ensure patients are aware of their appointments.
