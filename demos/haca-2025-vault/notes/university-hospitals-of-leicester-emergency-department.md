# University Hospitals of Leicester Emergency Department

University Hospitals of Leicester operates a single-site emergency department (ED) serving a population of over one million people. It is described as the largest single-site emergency department in Europe. Attendances have grown year-on-year, with exceeding 1,000 attendances per day becoming a regular occurrence.

The service operates within a context of significant health inequalities. Leicester City has a lower-than-average healthy life expectancy, particularly among disadvantaged communities, and is one of the most deprived local authorities. This contrasts with other areas served by the Leicester, Leicestershire, and Rutland integrated care system, which have high affluence. The only demographic within the system with above-average healthy life expectancy is white males living in the county. The city is ethnically plural with no ethnic majority and is increasingly youthful due to its university status. Challenges include health literacy and digital exclusion driven by literacy rather than technology access.
