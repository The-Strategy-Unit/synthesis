# RAP drop-in sessions

RAP (Reproducible Analytical Pipeline) drop-in sessions are fortnightly support meetings held on Wednesdays at 10:00 a.m., facilitated by the data science team within the NHS Strategy Unit. These sessions replaced similar offerings previously run by NHS England, which ceased due to funding and changing requirements around March or April of the year prior to the presentation. The sessions are open to anyone within the NHS, including analysts and managers, regardless of coding experience.

The format involves an hour-long call where participants can ask questions, seek guidance, and discuss challenges related to end-to-end data science processes, such as testing code, connecting scripts to databases, and creating accessible visualizations. The facilitators do not claim to have all answers but aim to facilitate discussion, help participants find others with similar problems, and share resources. Discussions are documented on GitHub, capturing questions and follow-ups. Common themes include upskilling (e.g., learning Python), deployment of tools like Shiny apps, and data safety concerns regarding personal identifiable information. The sessions also serve as a networking space for participants to connect and share code or ideas.
