# NHS England Regional Analytics Team

The NHS England Regional Analytics Team is a unit within NHS England comprising approximately 25 to 29 funded posts. Operating in a regional capacity, the team works closely with frontline services and within the wider health and social care ecosystem. Its primary function is to deliver advanced analytics while meeting tight operational deadlines.

The team has implemented specific strategies for upskilling and leadership development to manage stakeholder expectations and internal capacity. These strategies are designed to ensure that the team possesses the right skills at the right level, acknowledging that not every analyst needs to be an expert in all areas simultaneously. The approach aligns with broader ecosystem governance and is endorsed by senior leadership, including directors and chief operating officers.
