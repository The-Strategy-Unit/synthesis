# Virtual Fracture Clinic Challenges and Mitigations

Several challenges were identified during the Virtual Fracture Clinic (VFC) implementation. Not all patients were suitable for the VFC, and inappropriate referrals initially increased the workload on the on-call team. The study period was short, and there were missing waiting time data points.
<!-- synthesis-claim:6b82bd315b24dc6ec27c4ac7f0b6c4df7fb555fc0bf5aff704f875e8958e90af -->

Mitigation strategies included booking patients with language barriers for face-to-face appointments or using the DA language line service during phone consultations. Multiple drop-in sessions were organized to familiarize Emergency Department (ED) staff with criterion referral pathways. Posters were also used in A&E for quick referencing.
<!-- synthesis-claim:6b82bd315b24dc6ec27c4ac7f0b6c4df7fb555fc0bf5aff704f875e8958e90af -->

Future recommendations for the VFC include reducing the volume of planned face-to-face clinics and focusing the VFC exclusively on new cases. Ongoing staff education is recommended to ensure consistent referral pathways. Additionally, a longer duration retrospective study is proposed to assess long-term performance and address the limitations of the short initial study period.
