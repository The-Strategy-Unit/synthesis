# Operationalizing Data Model Delivery

Operationalizing the delivery of the demand capacity model required a strategic shift from an inclusive, consensus-driven approach to a more directive style with laser focus. Key strategies included adopting high humility and kindness to manage stakeholder relations, and creating a 'minimum viable' solution to meet the 7 December deadline.

Scope management was critical; the team renegotiated the initial target of 27 trusts to a phased approach to avoid overpromising. Strict prioritization of critical tasks over 'nice-to-have' features was enforced. The primary goal was to get stakeholders to correctly describe their processes, ensuring a working model could be delivered despite resource constraints and stakeholder resistance.
