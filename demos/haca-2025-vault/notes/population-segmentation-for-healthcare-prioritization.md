# Population segmentation for healthcare prioritization

Prioritizing healthcare resources requires looking beyond specific long-term conditions (such as diabetes, hypertension, obesity, COPD, and heart failure) to consider population segments. Data from North West London indicates that the greatest percentage of A&E activity comes from people with long-term conditions under the age of 65, closely followed by the healthy population under 65. This suggests that different population groups require different priorities and models of care, rather than a one-size-fits-all approach based solely on condition prevalence.
