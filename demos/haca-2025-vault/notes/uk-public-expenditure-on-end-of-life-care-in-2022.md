# UK public expenditure on end-of-life care in 2022

A study commissioned by Marie Curie and conducted in partnership with the Nuffield Trust estimated public expenditure on adults in their last year of life in the UK for 2022. The analysis covered healthcare, social care, and social security spending for the 652,000 adults aged 18 or older who died that year. The total estimated public expenditure was £22.1 billion, amounting to approximately £33,900 per person. The study aimed to inform current spending decisions by analyzing data from England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, focusing on expenditure rather than the effectiveness of interventions or carer impacts.
