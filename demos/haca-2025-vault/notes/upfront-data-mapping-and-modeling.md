# Upfront Data Mapping and Modeling

Upfront data mapping and modeling is a critical step in the Woodyard approach, involving significant planning before building data products. This process establishes the 'direction of travel' and allows for efficient planning by understanding what data assets already exist ('what was already in the yard'), identifying gaps, and determining what needs engineering or redesign. By conducting this work early, teams can ensure that a single underlying model is built to feed multiple products, preventing the creation of new bespoke solutions for every slightly different question.

This approach contrasts with ad hoc, one-off data extracts and emphasizes clarity regarding where logic resides and who owns specific parts of the data journey. It aims to reduce waste, improve flow, and ground business intelligence operations in craft. The focus on reusable data pathways and a single underlying model helps to scale data work effectively and avoid redundant efforts.
