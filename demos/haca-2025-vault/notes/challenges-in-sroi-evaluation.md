# Challenges in SROI Evaluation

Practitioners have identified several challenges and lessons when applying SROI. The guidance, such as the 90-page document from the Institute of Social Value, can be initially difficult and time-intensive to process. Avoiding double-counting proxies is also difficult. Key lessons include expecting the unexpected, as stakeholders often reveal unanticipated outcomes, highlighting the importance of multiple perspectives. It is crucial to focus on what can be measured and avoid forcing proxies for changes that do not fit.

Overclaiming must be avoided; simple counts of service users can misrepresent impact, so rigorous methods are necessary. Stakeholder engagement deepens understanding and strengthens credibility, especially when commissioning services. To improve accuracy, optimism bias should be accounted for, for example, by reducing outcomes by 25% to test if returns remain strong. Finally, descriptions of changes should be reviewed before looking at numerical output figures to prevent assumptions from being influenced by outcomes.
