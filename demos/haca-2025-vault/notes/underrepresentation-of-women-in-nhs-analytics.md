# Underrepresentation of Women in NHS Analytics

Women remain underrepresented in senior analytical roles across the NHS, with less than 30% of analysts in senior grades being women. This statistical gap is accompanied by qualitative challenges, including talented women feeling invisible in meetings, lacking confidence despite their expertise, and a lack of visible role models in senior positions. There is also a noted absence of safe spaces for discussing burnout, work-life balance, and the realities of working in analytics.

This underrepresentation is identified as a workforce gap that impacts the profession's capability and retention. The issue extends beyond mere numbers, reflecting deeper structural and cultural barriers that hinder the progression and well-being of women in the field.
