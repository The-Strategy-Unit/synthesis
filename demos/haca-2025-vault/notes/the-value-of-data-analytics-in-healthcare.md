# The value of data analytics in healthcare

Data analytics and data science are characterised as critical, life-saving disciplines that merit the attention of the "best minds of the generation." This perspective contrasts with the perception that top talent is often diverted to less impactful tasks, such as optimising ad clicks. The argument posits that robust analytics are essential for making evidence-based decisions, countering reliance on "policy based evidence" or beliefs that lack empirical support. The Goldacre review is cited as a relevant benchmark for these efforts; while some of its recommendations have been implemented, others remain outstanding, including the support for conferences like Hacker.

The synthesis emphasises that improving health outcomes requires not only technical proficiency but also sustained investment in the field and the integration of human judgment. It highlights the tension between the high value of analytical work and the challenges it faces, including negative media coverage and inflammatory commentary on professional platforms. Ultimately, the discipline is framed as vital for ensuring that healthcare decisions are grounded in data rather than assumption.
