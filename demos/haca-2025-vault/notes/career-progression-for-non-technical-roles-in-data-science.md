# Career progression for non-technical roles in data science

There is a recognized gap in career progression for non-technical roles within data science teams, such as product managers and project managers. While technical expertise is highly valued, roles focused on understanding users and managing projects often lack clear job descriptions, training paths, and advancement opportunities. These disciplines are described as being 'crowded out' by the focus on technical skills.

Addressing this requires the broader community to define these roles clearly, value the distinct skills they bring, and create structured career paths. Formally defining and separating product roles, such as product management, product design, and user research, from delivery roles can lead to more focused and effective outcomes. This structural clarity helps ensure that professionals in these areas have a clear direction for growth within the field, preventing role confusion and ensuring that the strategic direction and user needs are addressed independently from technical implementation.
