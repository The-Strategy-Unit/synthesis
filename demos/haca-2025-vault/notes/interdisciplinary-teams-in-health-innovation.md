# Interdisciplinary teams in health innovation

Effective health innovation projects, such as those within Health Innovation Networks, benefit from interdisciplinary teams combining diverse analytical skills. Key roles include evaluation managers skilled in qualitative analysis, data analysts with data science backgrounds, and project managers. This mix of paradigms and perspectives allows for a comprehensive approach to problem-solving, integrating behavioral insights, data science, and operational research.
