# NHS National Competency Framework Overview

The NHS National Competency Framework (NCF) is a skills and competency structure designed for professionals working in data and analytics within healthcare, public health, education, and other sectors. It articulates the skills required for various roles across five practitioner levels, aligning with professional body statuses while transcending the varying grade boundaries found in organisations such as the Civil Service, local authorities, and the NHS. The framework covers specific job families, including data analysts, data engineers, data scientists, data governance, data ethics, analytics engineers, and machine learning engineers, spanning from entry-level (Band 3) to senior leadership (Band 9).

Designed as a modular, adaptable tool, the NCF aims to provide consistent language for roles while allowing organisations to construct job descriptions relevant to specific needs. It prioritises immediate usability for the current NHS landscape over idealised structural separations. The framework supports benchmarking against a national peer-reviewed standard and facilitates training and career mapping through associated tools and communities. Unlike the Government Analytical Services (GAD) framework, which separates Business Intelligence (BI) and analysis, the NCF merges these roles at the core level, reflecting the reality that such separation is not currently standardised in NHS job descriptions. Core skills such as numeracy, digital data skills, and data transformation are identified as common to both roles, particularly at junior levels, with specialisms layered on top to differentiate specific types of analysts.
