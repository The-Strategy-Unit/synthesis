# Trends in asthma mortality in Dorset

Dorset's asthma mortality rate was previously worse than other Integrated Care Boards (ICBs) in the Southwest. Historical data up to 2019 showed a rate of 3.5 deaths per 100,000 of the population. Following the implementation of integrated proactive work and data-driven interventions, the mortality rate has dropped and continued to decrease, bringing Dorset in line with the rest of the Southwest. This improvement is estimated to save approximately 20 to 30 lives per year compared to previous practices. While a direct causal link is hesitant to be claimed due to complexity, the association between improved care management and reduced deaths is considered likely.
