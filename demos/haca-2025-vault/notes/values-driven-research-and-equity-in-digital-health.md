# Values-Driven Research and Equity in Digital Health

Jess Morley describes her research program as fundamentally values-driven, working backwards from core beliefs rather than determining values after selecting a topic. The primary value driving her work is equity and fairness. She asserts that everyone has an equal right not just to access care, but to achieve outcomes they themselves find desirable, rather than outcomes imposed by others. She views it as the responsibility of healthcare designers to facilitate this.

This perspective channels AI and analytics towards public health and collective good, contrasting with rhetoric focused on individual tech solutions. Morley distinguishes between using data for individual-level risk prediction and using it for population health modeling. The latter approach focuses on understanding system-level dynamics and identifying appropriate intervention points, rather than managing individual patient risk. This framework aligns with the capability approach to health, which she finds transformative for understanding health beyond mere clinical metrics.
