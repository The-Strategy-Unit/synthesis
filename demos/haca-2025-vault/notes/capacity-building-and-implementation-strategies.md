# Capacity building and implementation strategies

A method involving the payment of practitioner time within local authorities to utilize and embed analytical tools into existing systems and workflows. This approach aims to build internal capacity and independence among colleagues, ensuring that resources are not only provided by external teams but are also integrated into daily practice. It is part of a broader strategy to create learning materials, training programs, and open-access resources.

The project commits to following the open science framework, making all files, code, and analyses publicly available, including in local authority files. Beyond providing analytical insights as an embedded team, the project aims to create learning resources that enable colleagues to perform similar analyses independently. This approach supports capacity building and ensures that the analytical work can be sustained and replicated by practitioners after the project concludes.
