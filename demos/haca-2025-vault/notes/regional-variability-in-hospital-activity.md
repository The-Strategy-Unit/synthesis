# Regional variability in hospital activity

There is significant variation between Integrated Care Board (ICB) areas in both the rates of hospital activity and the changes observed over time. Data is presented using age and sex standardized rates per 100,000 of the population. While some areas experience large increases in admissions or bed days, others see decreases. For instance, Cornwall and the Isles of Scilly demonstrated a 23% reduction in admissions for frail older people, contrasting with the national average increase. However, this area still experienced an 8% increase in bed days, remaining below the national average. The report includes data at ICB, local authority, and provider levels to allow for detailed analysis of these variations and to help ICBs and providers understand opportunities to reduce inpatient activity and the scale of those opportunities.
