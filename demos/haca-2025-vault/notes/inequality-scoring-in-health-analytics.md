# Inequality scoring in health analytics

An inequality score is a measure developed to numerically identify variation in inequality across different demographic groups and metrics. It is described as a simple measure of variation that considers only statistically meaningful differences, banding the result between zero and one. This tool helps decision-makers identify where the greatest inequalities lie to prioritize focus areas. For example, when applied to long-term conditions across deprivation groups, severe mental illness (SMI) showed the greatest inequality, followed by COPD. This indicates potential for prevention driven by deprivation and informs strategists about the socioeconomic profile of the populations served by specific health services.
