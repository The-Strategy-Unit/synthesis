# Mental Health Rough Sleepers Programme

The Mental Health Rough Sleepers Programme was a healthcare initiative implemented between 2019 and 2023, funded with approximately £30 million by NHS England. It established 37 services targeting mental health issues among rough sleepers in areas identified as having high need, with many services supplementing this budget from other sources. Although primarily focused on mental health, the services often adopted a holistic approach, linking to other services and sometimes addressing physical health needs.

The programme's impact evaluation aimed to assess two key outcomes: increasing the accessibility of elective services for homeless people and reducing demand for acute services within this population. The evaluation faced significant methodological hurdles, including difficulties in identifying homeless individuals within national datasets and allocating areas to specific services. Furthermore, the staggered introduction of services across different time points created challenges for standard comparative analyses.
