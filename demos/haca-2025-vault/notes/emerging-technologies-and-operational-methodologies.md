# Emerging technologies and operational methodologies

New technologies and methodological shifts offer potential solutions to longstanding healthcare challenges. Ambient voice technology and real-time monitoring systems, such as those used by major cardiac providers in India, can predict adverse events like heart attacks up to six hours in advance, reducing nursing staff requirements by up to 50%. Similarly, integrating genetic data with lifestyle factors enables highly personalized preventive care strategies, allowing for the stratification of screening programs to reduce unnecessary interventions for low-risk individuals.

Methodologically, there is a call to move away from applying the high statistical bar of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) to operational performance. Other industries adopt a test-and-learn approach, iterating based on results rather than waiting for definitive proof. Adopting this iterative mindset could accelerate improvement in healthcare operations. Additionally, evidence suggests that scaling up primary care organizations could improve patient continuity through broader staff ranges and extended hours, provided the organizations operate efficiently.
