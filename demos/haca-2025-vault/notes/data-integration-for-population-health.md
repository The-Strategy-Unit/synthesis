# Data integration for population health

Integrated care data warehouses serve as a cornerstone for robust health needs assessments by linking diverse datasets. In North West London, the system integrates primary care data with secondary care, mental health, community services, and adult social care. Additional datasets include EPC certificates and UPRN (Unique Property Reference Number). This linkage allows for analysis based on population characteristics rather than service utilization alone. It also improves the quality of demographic data, such as deprivation, ethnicity, age, and sex, by linking back to patient GP records. This enables the correlation of long-term conditions from primary care with secondary care outcomes, such as unplanned bed day rates.
