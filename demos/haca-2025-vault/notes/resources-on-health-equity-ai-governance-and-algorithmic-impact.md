# Resources on Health Equity, AI Governance, and Algorithmic Impact

Several resources are highlighted for understanding the societal impacts of technology and health systems. The BMJ AI and Digital Health Governance Collection, edited by Morley, focuses on the governance, ethics, legal, and social implications of artificial intelligence in healthcare. It encourages a broad interpretation of AI, including simpler data analytics, and aims to bridge disciplinary silos. Submissions are welcomed from diverse backgrounds, particularly those offering practical examples of ethical implications in real-time deployment within hospital settings.

For broader insights, the 'Data Science for Health Equity' community is noted as a platform for individuals to build an online presence and connect with others in the field. Recommended podcasts include 'The Oral Apothecary,' which focuses on the health and care system with personal questions, and 'The Artificial Human' on BBC 4, which explores different angles of AI and datification. Two books are recommended for understanding the societal impacts of technology: 'Automating Inequality' and 'Weapons of Math Destruction,' both written by women and noted as heavy but essential reading for understanding the broader implications of data and algorithms.
