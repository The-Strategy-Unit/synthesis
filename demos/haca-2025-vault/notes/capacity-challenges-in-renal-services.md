# Capacity challenges in renal services

Current renal services face insufficient capacity to meet future needs, driven by a growing population and changing health profiles. Existing projections indicate a continuous increase in the incidence of patients requiring kidney replacement therapy. Without changes to infrastructure or care models, the system will not have the capacity to treat all patients. Simulation is proposed as a necessary tool to predict the effects of interventions on this complex system, allowing for the exploration of feedback interactions and interconnectedness that are difficult to assess through traditional planning methods. Interventions analyzed include primary prevention strategies, such as smoking cessation and weight loss drugs, to reduce inflow, increased diagnosis rates, and medication use, such as SGLT2 inhibitors, to slow progression.
