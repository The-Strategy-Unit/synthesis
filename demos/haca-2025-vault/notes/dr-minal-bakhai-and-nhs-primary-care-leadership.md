# Dr Minal Bakhai and NHS Primary Care Leadership

Dr Minal Bakhai is a practicing General Practitioner (GP) and serves as NHS England's director for primary care and community transformation. She leads major national programs, including Modern General Practice and the National Neighbourhood Health Implementation Programme. Dr Bakhai advises government internationally and was awarded an MBE for services to general practice. Her work focuses on translating policy ambition into action, particularly regarding the shift towards neighbourhood health models. She advocates for permissive leadership to dissolve bureaucracy and enable practitioners to do their best work, supporting the transition to value-based care and whole-person outcomes.
