# Proactive Outreach in Dorset

Proactive outreach in Dorset involved connecting services and establishing regular meetings to facilitate early intervention. This approach aimed to expand on remote monitoring using Internet of Things (IoT) sensors, although this was part of the initial plan rather than a confirmed outcome. The outreach included offering energy efficiency booklets to help heat-proof homes and collaborating with Dorset Police Service to include messaging around preventing fraud, addressing financial vulnerability. Partners reported seeing firsthand instances where support was provided sooner, preventing falls or other crises, rather than waiting for health service repercussions.

The project emphasized the importance of involving those with direct experience, such as patients, carers, and residents, through partners who routinely interact with them. This distinction highlighted the difference between designing the data infrastructure and actively engaging with communities to implement solutions. The collaborative efforts helped break down organizational boundaries and facilitated organic connections, such as charities inviting individuals to weekly forums, contributing to a shift towards prevention and community-based care.
