# Tom Smith's transition from manufacturing to NHS analytics

Tom Smith, Head of Activity Analysis and Forecasting at Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust, transitioned from a background in large-scale engineering and manufacturing, specifically with Toyota and Rolls-Royce, to NHS analytics. Holding a degree in mechanical engineering, Smith initially avoided coding due to early exposure to Fortran but later returned to programming via VBA and adopted R. His role involves understanding a complex organization of approximately 18,000 employees by connecting problematic questions with appropriate data skills and datasets. His team is divided into two halves: one focused on activity analysis and data science, and the other on quality, nursing staff engagement, and patient engagement. Smith applies lean manufacturing principles from his time at Toyota to healthcare analytics, emphasizing the distinction between waste, unevenness, and overburden.
