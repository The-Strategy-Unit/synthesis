# CKD progression modeling methodology

The first component of the hybrid renal care model uses system dynamics to simulate the progression of chronic kidney disease. Patients enter the model at CKD stage three, as earlier stages are difficult to detect and data governance dictates this starting point. The model distinguishes between diagnosed and undiagnosed patients, noting that diagnosis typically occurs at stage three, though some patients may progress to stages four and five while undiagnosed. A key mechanism modeled is that diagnosis and management can slow the progression rate from stage three to stage four compared to undiagnosed cases. The model tracks flows through disease stages, accounting for mortality at any stage and the eventual incidence of end-stage kidney disease requiring kidney replacement therapy. The output of this model—the rate of incidence into KRT—drives the input for the second discrete event simulation model.
