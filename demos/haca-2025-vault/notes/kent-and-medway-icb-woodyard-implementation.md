# Kent and Medway ICB Woodyard Implementation

Kent and Medway Integrated Care Board (ICB) is an NHS organization that implemented the Woodyard Model to address issues of waste and fragmentation within its business intelligence function. Prior to implementation, the ICB experienced redundant data processing across different teams handling similar topics, including children and young people (CYP), demand and capacity, primary care, and resilience. Siobhan Ligorish, Head of Data Visualization at the ICB, presented the implementation details at HACA 2025.

The implementation involved forming a cross-functional core group including Public Health Managers (PHM), Data Visualization (DV), and colleagues linked to primary care. This group met weekly, maintained a live chat, and treated the work as a distinct project with explicit roles such as product owner, process owner, technical owners, and subject matter experts. Key steps included conducting upfront data mapping and modeling to identify gaps and existing assets, such as the GP resilience matrix, and building one underlying model to serve multiple products.

The impact of the implementation included fewer parallel builds, clearer role definitions, better prioritization conversations, and a team culture that balances speed with quality. The approach also emphasized creating a psychologically safe and neurodiversity-aware environment, allowing team members to surface risks early and be honest about uncertainty.
