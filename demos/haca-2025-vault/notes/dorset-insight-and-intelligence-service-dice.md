# Dorset Insight and Intelligence Service (DICE)

DICE (Dorset Intelligence and Insight Service) is a dedicated data engineering, analytical, and data science team hosted by the NHS within the Dorset Integrated Care System (ICS). It operates a federated data platform that securely connects disparate datasets, breaking down organisational boundaries to enable a person-centred view of health and social care interactions. The platform provides a holistic overview of the Dorset population by integrating data from various touchpoints, including acute trusts, community healthcare providers, primary care GP practices, local authorities, emergency services, the ambulance service, the voluntary sector, and the Wessex Cancer Alliance.

The platform is pseudonymised to maintain anonymity but allows for re-identification to NHS numbers at the primary care network level for clinical action. DICE supports insights at multiple levels, from the ICB and primary care network (PCN) levels down to individual patient records. It aims to create meaningful cross-system insights by linking data, transforming it into actionable intelligence to shift care closer to the community in a proactive and preventative manner.

The service utilises tools such as PowerBI, analytical papers, and presentations hosted on a secure online platform to enable informed decision-making. The team employs a structured process beginning with co-design to build data foundations, followed by technical infrastructure development, underpinned by clear trust and governance procedures that treat information governance as an enabler. DICE works with partners across the ICS to deliver these capabilities.
