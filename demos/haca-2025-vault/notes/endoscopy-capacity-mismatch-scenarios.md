# Endoscopy capacity mismatch scenarios

Scenario testing within the endoscopy ODE model reveals significant asymmetries in how waiting lists respond to capacity shifts between upper and lower GI procedures. When capacity was reallocated from lower GI to upper GI procedures, the active and planned colonoscopy waiting lists increased significantly, with a peak rise of approximately 150 individuals. Conversely, reallocating capacity from upper GI to lower GI resulted in a less permanent increase in OGD waiting lists (peak increase of around 500 individuals) but a much greater decrease in colonoscopy waiting lists.
<!-- synthesis-claim:5f8a9f3001c62fc9f77b34cbb0c04528e28f8cfec304bc928b00befef68d8a6a -->

These findings suggest that colonoscopy waiting lists are more sensitive to capacity changes than OGD lists. Consequently, shifting capacity toward lower GI procedures may be more effective in addressing bottleneck problems and reducing overall waiting times. The model highlights the importance of dynamic resource allocation to mitigate the impact of demand fluctuations on specific service lines.
