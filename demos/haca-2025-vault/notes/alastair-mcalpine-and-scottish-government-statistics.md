# Alastair McAlpine and Scottish Government Statistics

Alastair McAlpine serves as the Chief Statistician for the Scottish Government, a role dedicated to ensuring the trustworthiness and quality of data usage across government operations. He previously led the development of the Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation (SIMD) and is the co-director of ADR Scotland. His professional philosophy posits that the role of a statistician extends beyond numbers to focus on people, using analysis and evidence to improve lives and support better policy decisions.
<!-- synthesis-claim:2dc66f67c8245a60dea26bb599bc905749cfcdfe51bd4a15b2f81a66560350b6 -->

McAlpine advocates for statisticians to leverage their independence to 'speak the truth to power,' a principle advised by Professor Anne Glover. This involves using the gravitas of the title to support analysts lower down the ranks whose findings might otherwise be ignored. By finding allies willing to echo these arguments, senior leaders can ensure critical data insights reach decision-makers. This approach emphasizes that statistical leadership is not merely about technical accuracy but about advocacy and ensuring that evidence drives meaningful change.
