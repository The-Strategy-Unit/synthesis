# Participatory mixed-methods study on NHS Health Checks

A study prepared by the public health team at Birmingham City University in collaboration with Birmingham City Council, reported in March 2025, evaluated the NHS Health Check programme through community engagement and participatory research. The project utilised a participatory action research model involving 10 community organisations and 193 participants (93 NHS health professionals and 180 community participants). Of the community participants, 153 were within the eligible age range (40–70 years), but only 45 reported having attended an NHS Health Check. The study employed a double-layer sampling strategy across ethnicity, gender, and age, and used framework analysis for qualitative data. Participants represented 10 global majority groups (Arabs, Bangladeshi, Black Caribbean, Chinese, Ghanaian, Indian, Nigerian, Pakistani, Somali) and White British groups. The average participant age was 52.11 years. The study aimed to bridge the gap between statistical analysis and real-world experiences to inform local commissioning and service design.
