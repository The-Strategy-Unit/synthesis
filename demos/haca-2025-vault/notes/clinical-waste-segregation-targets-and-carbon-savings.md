# Clinical waste segregation targets and carbon savings

Under the NHS clinical waste strategy, acute hospitals are targeted to segregate 60% of clinical waste for energy-from-waste disposal. The dashboard allowed the trust to monitor this metric closely. When waste is shifted from alternative treatment (which is more carbon-intensive) to energy-from-waste disposal, the trust saves 352 kg of carbon dioxide equivalent per ton of waste. This shift also results in significant cost savings, as energy-from-waste is cheaper. The implementation of targeted interventions based on dashboard data led to sites regularly beating the 60% target, causing a noticeable drop in the site's overall waste carbon footprint.
