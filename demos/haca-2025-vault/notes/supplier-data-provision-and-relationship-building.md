# Supplier data provision and relationship building

Obtaining carbon data usage information relies heavily on relationships with suppliers. Suppliers are expected to provide this information, often on invoices, but the quality and format of this data vary. The trust emphasizes the importance of pushing suppliers to provide data in easy-to-use formats. This aspect of the project highlights that technical solutions must be supported by proactive engagement and relationship building with external partners to ensure data availability and consistency.
