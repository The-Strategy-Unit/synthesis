# Scotland Medication Assisted Treatment standards

Scotland's Medication Assisted Treatment (MAT) standards are a national initiative aimed at reducing drug-related harms and death by ensuring people receive evidence-based treatment promptly. A key performance indicator is starting prescriptions within three days of a person asking for help. Monitoring these standards requires reliable, regular insight into performance, as yearly benchmarking reports are insufficient for immediate action.
<!-- synthesis-claim:522f92c51507a75fac0864487c96e46ca17eec831e993f6c011925f70f6cebf3 -->

Local Alcohol and Drug Partnerships (ADPs) and substance use services need weekly data to identify pressure points, assess if care is being accessed fast enough, and determine if immediate interventions are required. The standards drive the need for accessible, real-time local reporting to translate national goals into improved patient care. This context necessitates the development of automated reporting workflows that can provide timely, accurate data to frontline staff and managers.
