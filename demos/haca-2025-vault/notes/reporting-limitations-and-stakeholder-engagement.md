# Reporting Limitations and Stakeholder Engagement

Quarterly reporting in the National Lung Cancer Audit faces statistical limitations due to small numbers. When indicators are restricted to specific disease subtypes, stages, or performance statuses and reported at the trust level with quarterly time increments, the data points become very small. These data points must be suppressed to protect confidentiality, resulting in interruptions to time trends. Consequently, not all indicators from the annual report are feasible for inclusion in the quarterly report. The intended audience for these quarterly reports is NHS provider teams in England, as quarterly data is currently only received for England, not Wales.
<!-- synthesis-claim:d53f9a84f652ab515a3f17430fbe1487d6591881fa8369df030fc6bea4047a77 -->

To drive continuous improvement, a structured feedback loop was implemented involving diverse stakeholders. Internal meetings occurred weekly with the audit team, monthly with the Clinical Effectiveness Unit, quarterly with funders, and six-monthly with clinical reference groups and patient forums. Feedback was collated using a Kanban board to provide workflow visibility, prioritise features based on team capacity, and prevent bottlenecks. Focus group feedback indicated that the dashboards supported better data-driven decision-making for providers.
