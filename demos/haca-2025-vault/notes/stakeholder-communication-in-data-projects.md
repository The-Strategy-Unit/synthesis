# Stakeholder Communication in Data Projects

Effective stakeholder communication is a significant challenge in implementing new data operating models, such as the Woodyard Model. Stakeholders often have their own journeys and needs, sitting on different 'pillars' than the data team. Without proper engagement, the adoption of shared data benches and reusable pathways may fail, as stakeholders may not understand or support the new processes.

Effective communication requires taking time to have conversations and understand stakeholder perspectives to avoid 'pushing against the tide.' This involves helping stakeholders understand the benefits of the new model, such as reduced redundancy and improved insight quality, and addressing their specific needs and concerns. By fostering open dialogue and collaboration, data teams can ensure that stakeholders are aligned with the new operating model and contribute to its success.
