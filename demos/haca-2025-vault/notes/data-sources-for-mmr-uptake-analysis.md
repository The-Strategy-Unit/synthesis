# Data sources for MMR uptake analysis

Analysing MMR uptake involves navigating multiple data sources with varying accessibility, granularity, and limitations. COVER (Coverage of Vaccination Evaluated Rapidly) is publicly available and published quarterly but is limited to ages two and five, lacks ethnicity data, and is aggregated at GP practice or local authority levels. INFORM, published by the UK Health Security Agency, requires special access, covers ages 2–30 individually, but is restricted to GP practice level without ethnicity data.

The Federated Data Platform (FDP) offers near-real-time data for ages 1–26, including geography (LSOA, PCN, GP practice) and ethnicity, though availability varies by region. CHIS (Child Health Information System) provides near-real-time data for children aged 1–19, accessible via report requests, and allows aggregation by GP practice, LSOA, school, local authority, and ethnicity. Access to multiple data sources allows analysts to select the most appropriate metric for their specific question, compensating for the limitations of individual systems.
