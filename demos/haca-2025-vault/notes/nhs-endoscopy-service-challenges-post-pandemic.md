# NHS endoscopy service challenges post-pandemic

Post-pandemic, NHS endoscopic services faced mounting demand and constrained budgets, necessitating tools for optimizing resources. Data from the first three months of 2020–2021 indicated that only 57.9% of endoscopic services met urgent cancer waiting times, with even lower performance for routine and surveillance waits. A diagnostic test report highlighted that 28% of patients undergoing endoscopy services were waiting more than six weeks. These operational pressures underscore the need for advanced modeling techniques to address capacity mismatches between upper and lower GI services and improve patient outcomes.
