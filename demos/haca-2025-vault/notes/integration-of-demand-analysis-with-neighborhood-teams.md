# Integration of demand analysis with neighborhood teams

Future steps for demand analysis work involve overlaying findings with the development of integrated neighborhood teams. The goal is to inform how these teams, particularly city-level integrated neighborhood teams, can use population health insights to predict, manage, and respond to demand. This includes fitting demand and on-the-day operational management strategies into day-to-day patient flow management. The work represents a juncture where multiple directions are possible, requiring prioritization based on patient need and potential impact. This synthesis connects the analytical findings on demographic drivers and case mix with strategic operational responses in community care.
