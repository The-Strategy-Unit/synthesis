# Version 3 Updates to NHS Competency Framework

Version 3 of the NHS National Competency Framework introduces significant changes aligned with the Government Analytical Services (GAD) framework. Key updates include the introduction of a new 'Evaluator (Digital)' role, covering practitioner levels 1 through 5, with competencies in scoping, design, data collection, management, and dissemination of evaluation findings. Existing sections have been revised, notably the introduction of competency DAC7 for data analysts, which addresses data ethics and the professional standards surrounding AI use. The Data Ethicist role has been extended from levels 4 and 5 down to level 1 to include entry-level skills. Additionally, the behaviors framework has been expanded with competency BE7, focusing on reflective practice.

Version 3 also distinguishes between core competencies, expected of 90% of roles, and specialist technical skills that differentiate individual roles. In response to feedback, definitions for existing specialisms in advanced statistics and SQL have been updated. Three new specialisms have been introduced, largely informed by work with public health sectors: skills in responding to consultations, design and analysis of studies, and literature searching and critical appraisal of research results. These updates aim to make the framework more adaptable, targeted, and useful for training and recruitment within the health sector.
