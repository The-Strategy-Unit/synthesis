# Data Repatriation from Central to Local Providers

There is an identified imbalance where providers and Integrated Care Boards (ICBs) send significant amounts of money and data to central bodies but rarely receive it back. Advocacy exists for the repatriation of national Clinical Data Store (CDS) data to local levels to enable easier benchmarking and more effective local analysis. The lack of data return is attributed partly to procurement processes that ignore local data organisation needs, reinforcing the need for canonical data models to facilitate this flow. This repatriation supports the broader goal of ensuring that data expertise informs strategic decisions at the local level, rather than being locked away in central systems.
