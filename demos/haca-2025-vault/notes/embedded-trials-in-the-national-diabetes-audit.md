# Embedded Trials in the National Diabetes Audit

Two specific trials have been embedded within the National Diabetes Audit to test strategies for improving diabetes care. The first trial investigated whether providing a quality improvement collaborative to specialist diabetes teams would increase the use of insulin pumps for eligible people with type 1 diabetes. This study involved 77 teams, with half randomized to receive the collaborative immediately and the other half receiving it later. The assessment utilized routine audit data alongside qualitative methods, including interviews, observations, and document analysis, to evaluate effectiveness, cost-effectiveness, and implementation processes.

The second study, known as EQUIP2, focused on young adults with type 2 diabetes. It randomized all primary care networks in England into three groups: those receiving webinars, those receiving e-learning, and those receiving delayed facilitation. Like the first trial, EQUIP2 assessed the effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of these interventions using routinely collected data and mixed-methods evaluation. Both studies exemplify the integration of rigorous research design into national audit frameworks to inform clinical practice.
