# Virtual Fracture Clinic Workflow and Platform

The Virtual Fracture Clinic (VFC) utilised an integrated care system called Careflow. The workflow involved admin staff booking patients onto a clinic list where all relevant information, including histories and imaging, was available. Clinicians would review this information, propose a plan, and then contact patients via telephone, similar to a face-to-face clinic interaction. Following the consultation, a letter was dictated and sent to both the General Practitioner (GP) and the patient.

The VFC capped patient contacts at 25 per day. This limit was chosen based on advice from the British Medical Association (BMA), which suggests that around 25 patient contacts per day is a safe limit in primary care. Although described as conservative, this number was selected to reflect demand while ensuring safe and manageable workload levels for clinical staff reviewing referrals remotely.
