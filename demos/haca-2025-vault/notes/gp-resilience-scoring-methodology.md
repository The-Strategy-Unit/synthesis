# GP Resilience Scoring Methodology

The GP Resilience Matrix utilises over 40 indicators to assess practice health. These indicators are normalised using Z-score transformations, which measure how far a data point is from the mean in standard deviations. The mean can be set as a local benchmark (NHS Kenton Medway) or a national benchmark (England). Each indicator has three possible triggers: positive variation (more than 1.5 standard deviations from the mean in the desired direction), Level 1 (negative direction, between minus 1 and minus 1.5 standard deviations), and Level 2 (more than minus 1.5 standard deviations). Practices are banded into four categories based on the number of triggers attained: Higher Achieving, Achieving, Approaching Review, and Review Identified. Executive decision-makers can dynamically adjust criteria leniency and select specific metrics for scoring, which reranks practices. Contextual indicators such as CQC scores, deprivation scores, and ethnicity data are included but are not scored.
