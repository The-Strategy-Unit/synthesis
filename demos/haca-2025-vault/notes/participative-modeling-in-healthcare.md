# Participative modeling in healthcare

A modeling approach advocated by the Strategy Unit that involves engaging clinicians, nurses, commissioners, and patients throughout the development process. The goal is to build trust in the model by reflecting the real-world system back to stakeholders and ensuring they understand the construction and logic before interpreting outputs or 'what-if' scenarios. In the renal care project, this involved collaboration with lead stakeholders from the Midlands Renal Network, including Marie and Mark, and simulation expertise support from Anto at Loughborough University. This method aims to ensure that the resulting evidence is defensible and accepted by those responsible for implementing changes.
