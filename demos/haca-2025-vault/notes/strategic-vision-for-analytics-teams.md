# Strategic Vision for Analytics Teams

A strategic approach to defining the future state of an analytics team, focusing on having the 'right skills at the right level' at specific time points. This vision informs recruitment and retention strategies, aiming for analyst well-being and psychological safety. It emphasizes doing the 'right analysis to answer the right questions' rather than merely possessing data or capacity.

The vision is endorsed by senior leadership and aligns with broader ecosystem governance. It acknowledges that universal expertise in every domain is neither necessary nor practical. Instead, it supports distributed skill development across the team, ensuring that capabilities are matched to organizational needs while maintaining a sustainable workload for individual analysts.
