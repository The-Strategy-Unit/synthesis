# Unpaid Care Dashboard

Developed at the Center for Care, this dashboard aggregates census information to analyze intersectional profiles of unpaid carers. It allows breakdowns by various social characteristics and is fully automated. Users can enter email addresses to request statistics, PowerPoint presentations, and dashboard information directly.
<!-- synthesis-claim:e0bf58afc351024893434f8c2945370b10ec25384c36dcde44b1c4a27e0f61d9 -->

The resource includes cartoons and animations to aid interpretation and has seen uptake from charities and advocacy groups for reporting and business case development. It serves as a model for similar approaches in other topic areas, demonstrating how automated, accessible data tools can support sector needs and sustainability.
