# Feature importance in A&E waiting times

Analysis of feature importance in A&E waiting time models identified patient admission status and age as the most significant factors influencing waiting durations. Acuity was a more prominent predictor in the Python-based approach, while the arrival group, specifically emergency ambulance arrivals, was more significant in the R-based approach. Both methods indicated similar importance for time of arrival and discharge group assessment.

Crucially, the analysis found that ethnicity and deprivation indices did not significantly impact waiting times when other variables were controlled. Partial dependence plots visualised strong correlations between waiting time and age, admission flag, and acuity, as well as a link to the assessment stream. The lack of effect from ethnicity and deprivation provided reassurance regarding equity issues in A&E waiting times, suggesting that disparities observed in unadjusted data are likely confounded by clinical and demographic factors rather than direct discrimination in wait durations.
