# Priority areas for NHS analytical focus

Analysts are encouraged to focus intensively on a small number of analytically tractable projects that address enduring NHS problems. Key priority areas identified include expanding hospice provision to prevent unwanted hospital deaths and addressing structural funding challenges in the hospice sector, as highlighted by the National Audit Office. Another major focus is improving equity in access to hip and knee replacements for deprived areas. Potential solutions for equity issues include differential payments for providers working with deprived populations or outcomes-based contracts.

A significant focus is also delivering the 'left shift' in care, moving away from acute sector drift. This requires detailed, well-structured analysis rather than just business intelligence. Current guidance on strategic commissioning is criticized for its narrow focus on only two types of analysis: risk stratification and population segmentation. This limited view is described as odd and potentially harmful, particularly given a consensus shift against risk stratification, with references to Jess Morley’s review suggesting it often does more harm than good. It is argued that analysts should not let generalists dictate the methods used, and that a broader array of analytical techniques should be employed.
