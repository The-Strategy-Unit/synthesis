# Knowledge to Action cycle

The Knowledge to Action (K2A) cycle is a structured approach used to guide analytical projects from inception to impact, described as a continuous audit loop. The cycle consists of four key stages. **Scoping** involves engaging with customers at the very beginning to understand needs, issues, priorities, and opportunities. Crucially, this stage includes discussing the limitations of modern data and setting realistic expectations about what is possible. **Planning** determines what is feasible and explores multiple delivery options beyond simple dashboards or Excel sheets. **Implementation** executes the analysis, followed by **Evaluation**, which assesses the impact and effectiveness of the work.

The cycle emphasizes continuous engagement with stakeholders throughout the process. The goal is to create an 'intelligent customer' by fueling conversation between analysts and users early on, ensuring both parties are aligned on the problem and potential solutions. This approach moves analytical product development beyond static outputs to address burning questions through a cycle of building, testing, and developing. Evaluation is embedded throughout the scope, plan, and implementation cycle to seek improvements and understand user needs, fostering beneficial communication where analysts can support and advocate for product use.
