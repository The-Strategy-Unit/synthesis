# Sahlgrenska University Hospital ED prediction project

The Sahlgrenska University Hospital ED prediction project is a collaboration between the AI Competence Center at Sahlgrenska University Hospital in Gothenburg, Sweden, and the Clinical Operational Research Unit (CORU) at University College London (UCL). Funded by the Swedish Innovation Agency, the initiative began after clinicians at Sahlgrenska inquired whether AI could assist in managing unplanned emergency visits. The collaboration involved two extended onsite trips in February and May/June, alongside remote work.
<!-- synthesis-claim:5893a3a5d04acb23986f248cde88d079a1856ebd72852e6daffcd86ced004061 -->

The primary goal was to assess and evaluate the performance of an open-source predictive tool within the Sahlgrenska setting. The project successfully produced predictions for bed demand and patient inflow that were comparable to those generated at UCL Hospital, despite differences in data detail and hospital processes. This effort demonstrated that capacity constraints and overcrowding are common challenges across developed economies, challenging the perception that Scandinavian healthcare systems are immune to such pressures. The project served as a proof of concept for the generalizability of NHS-derived operational solutions.
