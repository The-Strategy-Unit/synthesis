# Technical Pre-Flight Checks and Support

To ensure reliable access and performance, the platform provides a device testing interface for presenters before they enter the main backstage or live environment. This tool checks the status of the presenter's camera, microphone, speaker system, and server connection. The purpose is to verify that all hardware and connectivity are functioning correctly, thereby reducing anxiety and technical failures during the live event. This step ensures that presenters can enter the backstage space with confidence that their equipment is ready.

For attendees or speakers who experience issues accessing the chat or joining a session, a specific support protocol is in place. Users are instructed to contact the support team, referred to as the 'hacker team,' or send an email. The support team will then send a direct invite link to the specific session. Clicking this link pulls the user directly into the backstage area, where the host can subsequently move them onto the live stream. This protocol serves as a 'backup plan to the backup plan' for technical difficulties, ensuring that access issues do not prevent participation.
