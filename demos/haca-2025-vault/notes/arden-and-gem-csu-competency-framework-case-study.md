# NHS Arden & GEM CSU Competency Framework Case Study

NHS Arden & GEM Commissioning Support Unit's Business Intelligence service, which supports more than 30 health and care organisations, used the competency framework to understand its workforce structure and support ongoing changes. It employed a self-assessment tool for individuals and add-on tools for managers to aggregate individual profiles. This allowed it to analyse skill composition, identify practitioner levels, and map strengths and gaps. The analysis helped structure discussions, identify areas for bespoke training (such as Python, Power BI, and data visualisation), and support staff in career pathways, including Level 4 data analyst apprenticeships. The framework is embedded in Personal Development Plan (PDP) conversations to help staff understand their core skills and development needs. This case study illustrates the practical application of the NHS Competency Framework in workforce planning and professional development.
