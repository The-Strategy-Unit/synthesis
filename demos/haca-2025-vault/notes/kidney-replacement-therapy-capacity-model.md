# Kidney replacement therapy capacity model

A discrete event simulation model designed to analyze patient flow through kidney replacement therapy (KRT) pathways and assess capacity implications at regional, national, and individual trust or centre levels. Patients enter the model from the CKD model upon developing renal failure. Pathways include conservative care (palliative), preemptive transplant (living or deceased donor), and dialysis (in-centre haemodialysis, home haemodialysis, or peritoneal dialysis). Patients may transition between modalities or proceed to transplant after dialysis. The model accounts for patient attributes, such as age and referral status, which govern probabilities of suitability for transplant, time in system, and mortality. It is used to evaluate interventions such as shifting patients from in-centre to home or peritoneal dialysis, increasing diversion to conservative care, improving preemptive transplant rates, increasing live donor availability, and reducing late emergency referrals.
