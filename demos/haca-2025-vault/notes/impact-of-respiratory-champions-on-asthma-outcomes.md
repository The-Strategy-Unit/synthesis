# Impact of respiratory champions on asthma outcomes

A comparison of two polar extremes in Dorset highlights the impact of dedicated resources. A best-performing PCN, which employed a full-time respiratory champion, achieved near-elimination of SABA use (down to 1.4% and falling) and a shift toward inhaled steroid use. This network had a hospital admission rate of 1.8% for asthma patients. In contrast, a less well-performing PCN without a respiratory champion showed no shift in SABA overuse, lower inhaled steroid uptake, and a hospital admission rate of 3.8%—more than double that of the champion-led network. This comparison suggests that targeted workforce intervention can significantly improve asthma management metrics. Across the 18 PCNs in Dorset, SABA overuse has fallen from 17% to approximately 10% over a three-year period, with significant variation between networks.
