# Culturally competent communication strategies

Redesigning communication strategies to be clear and culturally competent is essential for maximising the impact of public health spending. This involves identifying specific barriers and facilitators within diverse communities to ensure resources are allocated where most needed. Effective strategies include using appropriate languages, personalising invitations to increase perceived importance, and ensuring cultural sensitivity in service delivery, such as providing gender-concordant testers. Participants reported that standard invitation letters were often ignored because they were not targeted or personalised enough; recipients felt overwhelmed by generic mail. Suggestions for improvement included personalising letters, for example by addressing them by name to indicate importance. These changes aim to enhance accessibility and trust, moving beyond one-size-fits-all approaches to create interventions that are both evidence-based and culturally responsive.
