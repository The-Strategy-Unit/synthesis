# Hacker Conference for Health and Care Analysts

The Hacker conference is a national event for health and care analysts, described as being designed and delivered by analysts for analysts. The 2025 iteration marks the third occurrence of the conference. It is explicitly non-commercial, featuring no vendors or product sales, with the primary aim of fostering community, collaboration, and the sharing of analytical methods and findings. The event aims to centralise the role of analysts within health and care systems.

The conference originated from recommendations in Professor Ben Goldacre’s 2022 report, 'Better, Broader, Safer', which advocated for the development of analysts, the adoption of reproducible analytical pipelines, and the open sharing of analysis within the NHS. The 2025 event is organized by the Strategy Unit, an NHS team, with support from volunteers. Due to a lack of NHS funding for this iteration, the conference relied on financial support from the Health Foundation, which provided the platform, and HDR UK to avoid cancellation. Organizers emphasize the need for attendee feedback and advocacy to ensure the conference remains a fixed part of the analytical ecosystem.

The 2025 Hacker conference is themed 'Rising to the challenge: analysis at the heart of health and care systems of the future'. The programme is structured around four sub-themes: shifting from hospital to community, improving system flow and reducing waste, targeting care investment for better impact and value, and advancing analysis and professional development. While these themes guide the content, talks are not strictly segregated by theme.

The two-day virtual event features 1,150 registrations and a diverse range of formats, including eight keynotes, 20 insight sessions, 38 lightning talks, seven workshops, panel discussions, interviews, and posters. Ben Goldacaker is scheduled to close day one, and Andy Olowski will close the conference on day two. The planning team includes Chris Beely (hacker lead and head of the data science team), Rachel (Midlands analyst network lead), Mimmuda (communications lead), and support staff Natasha and Mache. Key deadlines, such as the submission of presentations and posters, were set for 24 November.
