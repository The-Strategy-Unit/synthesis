# Siobhan Ligorish

Siobhan Ligorish is the Head of Data Visualization at Kent and Medway Integrated Care Board (ICB). She played a key role in implementing the Woodyard Model within the organization, aiming to reduce waste and improve flow within the business intelligence function. Ligorish presented the implementation and its outcomes at the Hacker Conference 2025, detailing how the model helped transition the team from isolated efforts to a shared pathway for data processing and visualization. Her work highlights the importance of cross-functional collaboration and explicit role definition in achieving efficient data operations.
