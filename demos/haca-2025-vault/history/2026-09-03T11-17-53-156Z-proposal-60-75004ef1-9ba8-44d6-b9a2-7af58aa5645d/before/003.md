# Role of open source in healthcare data sharing

The use of open-source communities and reproducible pipelines is a significant enabler for the international collaboration and wider adoption of ED capacity models. Specifically, the R and Python ecosystems facilitate the sharing of methods for preparing real-time data for predictions, a process described as tricky and non-trivial. By sharing these technical workflows, organizations can lower barriers to entry for other teams looking to implement similar solutions. This openness encourages more widespread adoption and collaborative improvement within the healthcare sector, allowing institutions to build upon existing work rather than developing solutions from scratch.
