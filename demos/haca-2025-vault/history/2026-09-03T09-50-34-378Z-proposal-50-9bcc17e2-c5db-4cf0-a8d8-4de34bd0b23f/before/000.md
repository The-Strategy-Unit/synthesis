# Hacker Conference for Health and Care Analysts

The Hacker conference is a national event for health and care analysts, described as being designed and delivered by analysts for analysts. The 2025 iteration marks the third occurrence of the conference. It is explicitly non-commercial, featuring no vendors or product sales, with the primary aim of fostering community, collaboration, and the sharing of analytical methods and findings. The event format includes presentations, posters, panels, keynotes, workshops, and virtual connection spaces.

The conference originated from recommendations in Professor Ben Goldacre’s 2022 report, 'Better, Broader, Safer', which advocated for the development of analysts, the adoption of reproducible analytical pipelines, and the open sharing of analysis within the NHS. The 2025 event is organized by the Strategy Unit, an NHS team, with support from volunteers. Due to a lack of NHS funding for this iteration, the conference relied on financial support from the Health Foundation, which provided the platform, and HDR UK to avoid cancellation. Organizers emphasize the need for attendee feedback and advocacy to ensure the conference remains a fixed part of the analytical ecosystem.
