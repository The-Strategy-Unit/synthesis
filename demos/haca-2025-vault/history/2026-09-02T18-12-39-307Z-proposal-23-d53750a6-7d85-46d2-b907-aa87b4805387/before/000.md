# Data quality gaps in waiting list analysis

Significant data quality issues hindered the analysis of waiting list outcomes. Over 40% of pathways lacked recorded reasons for clock stops, making further analysis of removal reasons difficult. Additionally, linking waiting list data to secondary care records to track healthcare usage and specific procedures proved challenging. These gaps resulted in high levels of exclusions during analysis, which prevented many findings from reaching statistical significance and limited the ability to draw robust conclusions about the impact of waiting times.

The lack of detailed information on why patients are removed from waiting lists limits the understanding of the effectiveness of current management strategies. Similarly, the difficulty in linking data across settings hampers the ability to track the full patient journey and assess the true impact of waiting on health outcomes. Addressing these data quality issues is crucial for improving the reliability and usefulness of future analyses.
