# Professor Ben Goldacre's 2022 Report on NHS Analytics

Professor Ben Goldacre’s 2022 report, titled 'Better, Broader, Safer', is cited as the foundational document for the Hacker conference. The report contained numerous recommendations aimed at improving analytical practices within the NHS. Key proposals included the critical need to invest in the development of analysts, the adoption of reproducible analytical pipelines (RAPs) as a guiding principle, and the open sharing of analysis across the health service. The report also specifically recommended holding a conference to facilitate these goals and foster a collaborative analytical community.
