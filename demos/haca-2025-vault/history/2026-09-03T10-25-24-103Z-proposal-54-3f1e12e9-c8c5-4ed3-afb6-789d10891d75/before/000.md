# Dorset Insight and Intelligence Service (DICE)

DICE is a linked health and social care platform hosted by the NHS within the Dorset Integrated Care System (ICS). It provides a holistic overview of the Dorset population by integrating data from various touchpoints, including healthcare, local authorities, emergency services, and the voluntary sector. The platform is pseudonymised to maintain anonymity but allows for re-identification to NHS numbers at the primary care network level for clinical action. DICE supports insights at multiple levels, from the ICB and primary care network (PCN) levels down to individual patient records. It is supported by dedicated data engineering, analytical, and data science specialist teams working with partners across the ICS.
