# Raising the Analyst Voice

Raising the analyst voice involves moving analysts from the background, where they are often hidden behind complicated dashboards or developing unused techniques, to a position of influence in decision-making. This requires analysts to provide objective challenges to programmes, even when uncomfortable, ensuring that a proper analyst voice is present on every board. The goal is to ensure that data expertise informs strategic decisions rather than being overshadowed by clinicians or CIOs. This advocacy is supported by the Chief Data Analytical Officer Network, which facilitates connections and professional development for senior analysts.
