# Narrative-Driven Statistical Analysis

Narrative-driven statistical analysis involves moving beyond dashboards and raw numbers to communicate the human context behind the data. This approach aims to reshape thinking among senior executives and politicians by illuminating the challenges faced by populations. It emphasizes that analysis coupled with a compelling narrative is more effective at driving policy change than statistics alone. The concept advocates for statisticians to spend less time on repetitive analytical tasks and more time on communicating findings that reflect lived experiences.

This method involves finding stories within the data and co-creating narratives with community members or those who understand the context, using language that resonates with the subjects. For example, metaphors like the 'broken kettle' are used to make statistics relatable and coherent for policymakers. The goal is to counter the tendency of leaders to hide behind high-level indices and heat maps, instead making them confront the human cost of statistics. By telling stories alongside producing numbers, analysts can build trust and ensure that data serves to improve lives rather than just describe them.
