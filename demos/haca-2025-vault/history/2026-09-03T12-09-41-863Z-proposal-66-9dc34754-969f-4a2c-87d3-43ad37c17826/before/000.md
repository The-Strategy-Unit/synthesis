# Making Data Count Methodology

NHS England's 'Making Data Count' methodology is integrated into performance dashboards to provide assurance and variation analysis. This approach uses Statistical Process Control (SPC) charts, including fixed-date national standards and adjustable interval periods (e.g., Western Australia date-adjustable visuals). It allows users to assess metrics against the latest 15 days, 15 weeks, or 15 months to identify assurance and variation concerns. The methodology supports the creation of bespoke reports and quick insights on performance and statistical variation, helping users distinguish between common cause and special cause variation in healthcare metrics.
