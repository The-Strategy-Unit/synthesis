# Multi-level culture for analytical workforce development

Developing the analytical workforce requires a cultural shift across four distinct levels: individual, team, organization, and system. At the **individual level**, analysts should be driven to succeed, seek personal development, step outside their comfort zone, and network. At the **team level**, culture should prioritize growth and development over simple job allocation, encouraging stretching and challenging tasks even if failure is a risk. This environment supports the transition from isolated technical work to broader engagement.

At the **organizational level**, skills should be collaborated across departments through apprenticeships, secondments, and modern IT practices that enhance rather than impinge on analytical ability. At the **system level**, initiatives like the national competency framework, government statistical service badging, and continuous professional development (CPD) help define career paths. These systemic structures allow analysts to remain technical experts rather than solely becoming managers, supporting long-term retention and expertise within the field.
