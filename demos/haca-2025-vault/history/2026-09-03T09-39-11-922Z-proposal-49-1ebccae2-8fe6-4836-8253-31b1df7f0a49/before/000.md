# NHS Analytical Strategy and Workforce Challenges

The NHS analytical community faces significant challenges stemming from organizational reorganizations and resource shrinkage, which create uncertainty for analysts. Delivering analytically informed decisions with reduced resources requires increased national investment in analyst development and a new approach to work. This approach involves embracing analytical specialization, effective collaboration, and securing economies of scale by avoiding duplication of efforts.
<!-- synthesis-claim:be3d7a727aba2e600d4425f0d39db221f3a30fce637eeb13e13b982ffef91a4d -->

A proposed solution gaining momentum is the creation of a national improvement network for analyst teams, referred to as a 'decision support network'. This network aims to address the structural challenges facing the workforce and improve the efficiency and impact of analytical work across the health service.
