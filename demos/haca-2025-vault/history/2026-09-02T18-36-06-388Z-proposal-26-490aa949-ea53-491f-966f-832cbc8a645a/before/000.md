# Network Data Lab and Discover dataset

The Network Data Lab is a collaboration commissioned by the Health Foundation, uniting analytical teams across five UK labs. The Northwest London lab, comprising Imperial College Health Partners, Imperial College London, and the Northwest London Integrated Care System, focuses on deepening the understanding of factors affecting health. A defining feature of the project is its strong Patient and Public Involvement and Engagement (PPIE), where patient and public partners assist in scoping research questions, reviewing statistical analysis plans, and providing feedback on interim results.

The lab utilises the Discover dataset, a linked healthcare database covering approximately 2.9 million current patients and 2 million previously registered patients in Northwest London. This dataset integrates data from primary care (observations, prescriptions, appointments), secondary care (A&E, inpatient, and outpatient services via SUS data), community mental health, social care, and waiting lists. Access is governed by a data access committee that evaluates projects based on public benefit. For research purposes, a de-identified version is used, retaining patient keys for linking journeys while excluding identifiable data. The technical stack for analysis involves SQL for data extraction from Snowflake databases and R for statistical analysis and visualisation, with code made available on GitHub.
